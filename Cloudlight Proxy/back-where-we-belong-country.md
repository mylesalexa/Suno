## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** winter finally breaks, and the whole town has been waiting for one weekend — the fair comes
to town, the Ferris wheel goes up over the same four blocks it always does, and everyone who only shows up
once a year (old sweethearts, high school buddies, family scattered across the map) is suddenly all in one
place again. **Why it works:** "we don't ask for much, just a little summer" is the kind of gracious,
grateful hook that turns an ordinary hometown fair into something worth getting emotional about — pure
homecoming warmth, no edge to it at all.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre (full description, as provided):** country singalong anthem in the warm, front-porch-meets-festival
lane — four-on-the-floor groove, raspy heartfelt male lead, huge friendly gang-vocal chorus, acoustic
guitar, fiddle, banjo, light electric, hand-claps, big crowd singalong, feel-good grateful summer-night
production.

- **Tempo:** ~**112 BPM** (warm and easy, not rushed).
- **Vocal:** **raspy, heartfelt male lead** — huge friendly gang-vocal chorus.
