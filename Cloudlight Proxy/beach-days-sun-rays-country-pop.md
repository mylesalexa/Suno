# Beach Days, Sun Rays *(country-pop, light beach breeze)*

*A sunshine country-pop summer banger — school's out, calendar's in the trash, nothing but beach days and
sun rays. **Country-forward** now (acoustic, electric, pedal steel) with just a *light* beach breeze —
subtle steel-drum accents and a breezy lilt instead of a full island groove. Young, bright tenor
(Hunter-Hayes-style). Giant "oh-oh" singalong. Female swap noted. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Revision history:** country-pop → went full tropical → **pulled the tropical back** so it reads country
> first, with only a hint of beach breeze (a few steel-drum accents). Same school's-out hook & lyrics.

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the bell rings, summer detonates, and the whole season is one bright carefree blur of sun and
saltwater. **Strongest angle:** pure dopamine — a country-pop crossover that *sounds* like sunshine, built
on a chant ("school's out till September, beach days and sun rays") that's a caption, a hook, and a crowd
shout all at once.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** sunny **country-pop** summer anthem — country at the core (acoustic + electric guitar, pedal
steel) with pop hooks (claps, big "oh-oh" chorus) and just a *light* beach breeze (a few subtle steel-drum
accents, a breezy lilt). Country first, beach second — not full tropical.

- **Tempo:** ~**116–120 BPM** — bright, danceable, top-down.
- **Key:** **G major** (sunny), optional lift to **A** for the final chorus.
- **Vocal:** **young, bright male tenor** — Hunter-Hayes-style (youthful, boyish, a little higher and
  clean — right at home on a school's-out song). Energetic, a grin and a little twang so it stays country;
  conversational verses into a huge melodic chorus with stacked "oh-oh" harmonies. No heavy autotune.
