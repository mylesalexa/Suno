# Beach Days, Sun Rays *(country-pop crossover)*

*A sunshine country-POP crossover summer banger — school's out, the calendar's in the trash, and it's
nothing but beach days and sun rays. Brighter and poppier than the country clock-out anthem "Gone Till
September": snaps, claps, four-on-the-floor, a tropical groove, and a giant "oh-oh" singalong. Built to be
the carefree, save-and-repeat song of the summer. Male lead (female swap noted). Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **New concept / distinct lane:** where "Gone Till September" is the working-man's *clock-out* country
> anthem, this is the *beach-pop* crossover — same season, totally different sound and feeling (carefree
> sun-and-sand pop, not grind-escape). Title is its own hook: **Beach Days, Sun Rays.**

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the bell rings, summer detonates, and the whole season is one bright carefree blur of sun and
saltwater. **Strongest angle:** pure dopamine — a country-pop crossover that *sounds* like sunshine, with
a chant ("school's out till September, beach days and sun rays") that's a caption, a hook, and a crowd
shout all at once. No story to follow, no mood to manage — just press play and it's instantly summer.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** country-pop crossover summer anthem — country DNA (acoustic, banjo, a little twang) fused with
modern pop production (finger-snaps, claps, four-on-the-floor kick, bright "beachy" electric, tropical
lilt, stacked "oh-oh" hooks). Sunny, glossy-but-warm, radio + playlist ready.

- **Tempo:** ~**118–122 BPM** — bright, danceable, top-down.
- **Key:** **G major** (sunny), optional lift to **A** for the final chorus.
- **Vocal:** **male lead** — warm, easygoing, a smile in it; conversational verses into a huge melodic
  pop-country chorus. Stacked gang "oh-oh" harmonies + claps. Light pop sheen is fine; no heavy autotune.
