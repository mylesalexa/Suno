# Beach Days, Sun Rays *(pop / hip-hop version)*

*The Billboard-Top-40 flip of the school's-out summer hook — melodic rap verses over 808s and trap hats,
exploding into the same big sung "beach days and sun rays" pop chorus. Modern pop/hip-hop crossover summer
banger: snaps, claps, bright synths, young male vocal that raps the verses and sings the hook. Cloudlight
Proxy.*

> **Same hook, new genre:** keeps the "school's out till September, beach days and sun rays" chorus, but the
> verses are now rhythmic melodic rap and the production is 808 + trap-pop (think current Top-40 summer
> pop-rap). A touch of acoustic guitar keeps a thread of the country DNA without making it a country song.

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the bell rings and summer detonates — told with a fast, fun, melodic-rap flow over a Top-40
beat, landing on a giant sung pop chorus. **Strongest angle:** it's the *radio/TikTok* version — rap
verses pack in the summer details and momentum, the sung hook is pure dopamine, and the "oh-oh" tag is the
short-form loop. Built for the pool party, the cookout, the For-You page.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** pop / hip-hop summer crossover — 808 bass, crisp trap hi-hats, snaps and claps, bright plucky
synths, a light acoustic-guitar loop for warmth; **melodic rapped verses** into a **big sung pop chorus**
with stacked "oh-oh" hooks. Glossy, energetic, current Top-40.

- **Tempo:** ~**100–104 BPM** (half-time trap feel that still bounces); **Key:** G major (sunny).
- **Vocal:** **young male** — fast, melodic, conversational flow on the verses (a little auto-tune sheen is
  on-genre here, unlike the country cut), then full and bright singing the chorus. Optional female ad-libs
  on the post-chorus.

**Tip:** the contrast is the magic — keep the **verses rapped/rhythmic** and the **chorus fully sung** so
the hook lands like a lift. 808 + trap hats up front; a light acoustic loop keeps a thread of warmth. The
"(oh-oh) sun rays / beach days" tag is the short-form loop. If it feels too sparse, double the hats in the
chorus; if too busy, drop the synth in the verses and let the 808 + vocal carry.
