## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** last bell of senior year, the crew, one summer before everything changes — over a danceable
EDM/pop-hip-hop beat with a chant-and-move hook. **Strongest angle:** clean enough for the grad party and
the school feed, cool enough that a 17-year-old films it, and now **groovy enough that you can't stand
still** — the funky bass and the EDM drop do the boogie for you.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** clean, danceable **EDM / pop-hip-hop** summer anthem — four-on-the-floor club beat, **funky
boogie bassline** (disco-funk bounce), synth-pluck hook, an EDM **build-and-drop** on the dance break,
crisp trap hi-hats, claps; confident melodic rap verses into a huge sung chorus. Made to move, made for short-form.

- **Tempo:** ~**112–116 BPM** — club-bounce dance tempo (the four-on-the-floor + funky bass = the boogie).
- **Key:** **G major** (sunny).
- **Vocal:** **young male (late-teen)** — confident, smooth flow on verses, big and bright on the chorus,
  punchy call-outs on the drop. Gang-vocal shouts on the hook. Clean. No childish delivery.

## 🕺 The TikTok Dance (clean 8-count — biggest hit lands on the EDM drop)
On the **`[Dance Break]` drop** and the **"sun rays / beach days"** loop:
1. **"Sun rays"** — both arms shoot up into a V (sunshine), 2 counts.
2. **"Beach days"** — surfer sway, hips side-to-side, 2 counts.
3. **"Step-splash"** — step + kick a little "splash," 2 counts.
4. **On the DROP** — big "throw it up" + freeze + finger-guns, 2 counts (this is the money frame).
Loop it. The funky bass carries the groove between the moves; the drop is the snap.
