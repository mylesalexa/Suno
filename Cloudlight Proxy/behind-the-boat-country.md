# Behind the Boat *(neotraditional Texas country — Ella-Langley lane)*

*A fiery, fun, female-led summer LAKE banger about kneeboarding — strapping in, landing your first 360,
jumping out past the wake, water wars with your sister, eating it and coming up laughing, riding into the
endless horizon. Built in the Ella-Langley neotrad-Texas-country sound: fiddle, slide guitar, Telecaster,
two-step drive, Southern grit + modern radio polish. Chart-aimed. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Sound target (Ella Langley / "Choosin' Texas" lane):** slide guitars, two-step references, twang +
> Country-radio polish, rowdy fiery female attitude. We keep that grit but make it an *upbeat, joyful*
> lake banger instead of a heartbreak. Original lyrics; no artist names in the Suno box.

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the pure adrenaline joy of kneeboarding behind the boat with your sister/best friend — fear,
the spin, the wipeout, the laughing, the freedom. **Strongest angle:** it's a *girl-fearless* anthem on
the water — landing the 360 and screaming at the sky, ride-or-die "hold the rope" with your sister — with
enough specific lake detail to feel real and enough swagger to chart. Fun, rowdy, and clean.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** neotraditional **Texas country** — fiddle, slide guitar, Telecaster twang, driving two-step,
Southern grit with modern Country-radio polish; big, rowdy, catchy singalong chorus.

- **Tempo:** ~**138–146 BPM** — upbeat, driving two-step (a banger, not a ballad).
- **Key:** **D major** (bright, twangy).
- **Vocal:** **fiery young female** — gritty-sweet, confident, big belt on the chorus, a little rasp and
  attitude (rowdy Texas-woman energy). Real twang. Clean. **Female lead (male vocals excluded).**

the female lead (regenerate if it slips). If it's too soft, push the `two-step drive` and add `fiddle solo`
on the bridge; if too retro, add `modern country radio mix`.
