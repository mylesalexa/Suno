# Closing Time (Auto Shop) *(clean pop/hip-hop — "Patio Nights" groove)*

*An end-of-the-workday anthem for closing down the auto shop — pulling the bay doors, flipping the sign to
CLOSED, hanging the keys, grease on your hands and a porch light waiting at home. Captures the famous
"closing time" *feeling* (time to go, a little bittersweet, but home's on the other side) on the same clean,
smooth pop-hip-hop groove as **Patio Nights**. All-original lyrics. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Inspiration, not imitation:** it evokes the vibe of the classic "closing time / time to go home" anthem
> — but every line is original (no lyrics lifted). The hook word "closin' time" is a common phrase; the
> bittersweet "every goodbye opens a door home" sentiment is rebuilt in my own words for an auto shop.

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the satisfying ritual of locking up your own shop at the end of an honest day — last car
out, lights down, and the quiet pride of a place you built, with home waiting. **Strongest angle:**
universal working-person catharsis on a smooth groove — relief, pride, and that "see you tomorrow" warmth.
Clean, grown, broadly relatable (every small-business owner and tradesperson lives this).

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** clean **pop / hip-hop / R&B-pop** — smooth bounce, warm chords, finger snaps, mellow 808, lush
harmonies; **melodic sung-rap verses** into a big sung "closin' time" hook. Late-evening, radio-polished.

- **Tempo:** ~**100–104 BPM** — smooth head-nod bounce. **Key:** **A minor** (warm, reflective).
- **Vocal:** smooth, confident **male** — melodic sung-rap on verses (tuneful, pitched, not monotone),
  full and warm on the chorus, stacked harmonies. Clean. (Female/duet swap noted.)

## ⭐ Best Suno prompt
```
Clean pop hip-hop, 102 BPM, key of A minor, smooth bounce, warm chords, finger snaps, mellow 808, melodic sung-rap verses, big sung hook, lush harmonies, end-of-day R&B-pop, radio polished
```
**Tip:** same recipe as *Patio Nights* — keep it **smooth, head-nod, grown** (snaps + warm chords + mellow
808), `melodic sung-rap verses` so it's not flat, and let the "closin' time" hook open up big and warm.
The garage-door rumble + lock-click SFX in the intro/outro sell the shop. If it drifts country (the shop
imagery can pull it), keep `country` in Exclude and push `808, R&B-pop`.

## Variations
- **Female-lead / duet:** swap `male` → `smooth female vocal` (change "my girl" → "my love").
- **More soul:** add `neo-soul chords, Rhodes piano`. **More bittersweet:** slow to 96 BPM for a reflective cut.
