# Closing Time (Collision Shop) *(Nickelback-style post-grunge rock)*

*An end-of-the-day, blue-collar **rock anthem** for a modern collision repair shop — pulling frames,
matching paint, calibrating cameras, fighting the adjuster, and handing it back "like it never happened,"
then slamming the bay doors at closing time. Gritty, raspy, anthemic post-grunge in the Nickelback /
arena-rock lane. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Changed:** flipped from the clean pop/hip-hop cut to a **Nickelback-style rock** track, and rewrote the
> lyrics for a *modern collision/body shop* (frame machine, paint booth, ADAS calibration, supplements,
> rentals). Hard-working pride + a big "closin' time" rock hook. No artist name in the Suno box (filtered);
> the *sound* is described instead.

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the grind and the pride of a body shop — wrecks roll in folded up, you pull 'em straight,
paint 'em, calibrate 'em, give people their life back "like it never happened," then close it down loud.
**Strongest angle:** a working-man arena-rock anthem (the lane Nickelback owns) — gritty verses, a fist-up
chorus, and that satisfying end-of-shift release every tradesperson feels.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **post-grunge / hard rock** — heavy distorted power-chord riffs, crunchy drums, big anthemic
arena-rock chorus with gang backing vocals; gritty, raspy male baritone. Polished radio-rock, not metal.

- **Tempo:** ~**108–116 BPM** — driving, crunchy mid-tempo.
- **Key:** **D** (drop-tuned feel), big major-lift chorus.
- **Vocal:** **gritty, raspy male baritone** (post-grunge growl, melodic not screamed), full-throated on
  the chorus; **gang "whoa-oh" backing vocals** on the hook. A little rasp/grit is the whole point.
