## Long bio (about page / press kit)

Cloudlight Proxy is a songwriting project built on one simple idea: the stories are real, even when the
voice is borrowed.

Every song starts the same way — with a life. The long-weekend campfires and lake days, the faith that
holds steady when the world feels like it's shaking, the daughters and the dog and the love that outlasts
the headlines. **[Artist Name]** writes the words, shapes the characters, and steers every creative
decision; the melodies, arrangements, and performances are generated with AI, treated like a studio band
and a session vocalist rather than a replacement for the heart behind the song. The lyrics are human. The
direction is human. The *reason* the song exists is human. The AI just helps it sing.

That approach lets the catalog roam without losing its center. One track is anthemic, faith-driven
pop-punk — gang vocals, driving guitars, hope shouted into the dark. The next is warm, 90s-leaning country
— fiddle and pedal steel, a story you can see. But it all circles back to the same handful of things:
family, gratitude, the people gathered around the fire, and the One who holds it all together.

The name says it plainly. Cloudlight Proxy is light finding its way through the clouds, carried by a
proxy — a generated voice standing in for a real one, so the moments behind the music can travel farther
than they ever could from a back porch or a kitchen table. It's not pretending to be something it isn't.
It's a new kind of folk tradition: real people, real stories, handed to a machine to give them a melody —
and handed back to the people they were written for.

## Short bio (Spotify / Apple Music profile)

Cloudlight Proxy turns real life into song — campfires and lake days, faith and family, love that
outlasts the noise. The lyrics, stories, and creative direction are all human; the music is composed and
performed with AI, used like a studio band to give honest, hand-written songs a voice. The result spans
heartfelt country and anthemic faith-pop-punk, but always lands in the same place: gratitude for the
people and moments that matter most.
