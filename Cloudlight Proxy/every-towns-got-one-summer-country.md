# Every Town's Got One — Summer Banger Package

*Upbeat modern country summer anthem — the FUN flip of "Gas Station at Midnight." The gas station isn't
lonely; it's the launchpad: pre-party, after-party, meeting point, where every summer night starts and
ends. Big chantable chorus built on "every town's got one." Male lead, windows-down, tailgate energy.
Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## 1. Short Song Concept

Every small town has *that one* gas station — and on a summer night it's the brightest, busiest, best
place in the county. It's where the trucks line up before the lake, after the fair, after the game, after
the bar; where the cashier knows everybody, the cooler's already loaded, and somebody's always yelling
"where we goin' next?" The neon out front is a "little neon sun" that keeps the night going after the real
one's gone down. No heartbreak — just the universal, joyful truth that *every town's got one*, and that's
where everybody goes.

## 2. Suno Style Prompt (under 200 chars)
```
Upbeat modern country summer anthem, 135 BPM, key of G, driving acoustic and electric guitar, banjo, claps, tailgate drums, warm male lead, huge gang-vocal singalong chorus, feel-good windows-down
```

## 3. Longer Suno Prompt (under 500 chars)
```
Upbeat modern commercial country summer anthem, ~135 BPM, key of G. Windows-down, tailgate-party energy — Luke Bryan / FGL / summer-Wallen lane. Warm, easygoing male lead with a grin in it; fast conversational verses exploding into a huge, chantable gang-vocal chorus with a hands-in-the-air post-chorus. Driving acoustic + bright electric guitar, banjo rolls, fiddle, hand-claps, four-on-the-floor backbeat drums, thick bass, stacked harmonies. Feel-good, nostalgic, flirty, radio-ready. Organic, no autotune. Build into a key-lift final chorus.
```

# THE PANEL — "Squeeze It Into a Banger"

*Same five judges, scoring for a SUMMER ANTHEM this time (so the weight shifts: Hook, Singability, and
Energy/Vibe matter most; "Emotion" naturally runs lower for a party song — that's fine). Each judge gives
a score and one concrete squeeze note.*

| Judge | Hook | Sing | Energy | Fit | Orig | Total | Squeeze note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rae** (songwriter) | 10 | 10 | 9 | 9 | 7 | **45** | "'Little neon sun when the real one's gone' is the keeper line — make sure it lands every chorus." |
| **Dwight** (radio PD) | 10 | 10 | 10 | 9 | 7 | **46** | "Title-in-hook, chantable, tests anywhere. Add the (Hey!) post-chorus on the *first* chorus too — don't make 'em wait." |
| **Hank** (trad) | 8 | 9 | 9 | 8 | 7 | **41** | "Keep the banjo and a fiddle real; one fiddle lick after the hook keeps it country, not pop." |
| **Mia** (curator) | 10 | 10 | 10 | 9 | 8 | **47** | "'Twenty on pump three, crushed ice in a foam cup' is the TikTok open. Lead the song on that line — it's the scroll-stopper." |
| **Loretta** (vocal coach) | 9 | 10 | 9 | 9 | 7 | **44** | "Make the post-chorus a true call-and-response so a crowd can scream it back — that's the festival moment." |

**Total: 223/250** — and on a *summer-anthem* curve (discounting Emotion, which a party song isn't trying to win), it's the highest-scoring up-tempo cut on the board, beating the old uptempo entry **V2 "Last Light in Town" (185)** comfortably. The only soft spot is **Originality** (gas-station party is a familiar lane) — but that familiarity is *exactly* what makes it instantly singable, so the panel calls it a fair trade.

**Panel consensus:** *"This is the summer single. It does the one thing a banger has to do — you can sing
the chorus before it's over. Tighten the front and the chant and it's radio-ready."*
