# Family Ski Trip Gong Show *(comedic country hootenanny)*

*A fast, funny, rowdy family-singalong country romp about the total chaos of the annual family ski trip —
everybody scattered, nobody knows where anybody is, accidental black diamonds, can't-make-it-down-the-hill,
ski-patrol toboggan rescues, $19 hot chocolate, and somehow it's still the best week of the year. Clean,
big gang-vocal chorus. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Vibe:** think a stomp-clap country hootenanny (fiddle + banjo + a whole family yelling the chorus) —
> a comedy story-song that's actually catchy. Affectionate chaos; everybody's been on this trip.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** upbeat **comedic country hootenanny** — driving acoustic, fiddle, banjo, stomp-clap, upright
bass; rowdy lead with a whole **family gang-vocal** chorus and shouted call-outs. Fast, funny, singable.

- **Tempo:** ~**148–152 BPM** — fast, rowdy, barn-burner.
- **Key:** **G major** (bright, hoedown).
- **Vocal:** **rowdy male lead** with comedic timing + **family/gang vocals** shouting the hooks and the
  "Pizza! French fry!" call-outs. Clean.
