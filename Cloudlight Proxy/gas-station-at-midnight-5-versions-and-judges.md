# Gas Station at Midnight — 5 Versions + Judges' Panel

*Five distinct takes on the same concept (the gas station as "the last light in town"), spanning fast to
slow, traditional to contemporary — then a five-seat country judging panel scores them and picks the
winner. Each version is Suno-ready (style box ≤200 chars + full lyric). Cloudlight Proxy.*

## Version 3 — "Pump Number Two" *(slow, stripped ballad — the one who's leaving)*
**Feel:** intimate, aching, tearjerker. First person, sitting under the sign deciding whether to go.
**Tempo/Key:** ~70 BPM, C major.

**Style of Music box**
```
Slow country ballad, 70 BPM, key of C, piano, acoustic guitar, weeping pedal steel, brushed drums, intimate warm male lead, emotional and tender, big aching chorus, sparse, no autotune
```

**Lyrics**
```
[Verse 1]
Full tank, packed truck, foot on the brake
Last stop on the only road I ever take
The dash clock says it's midnight, the sign says I should go
But my hands won't leave the wheel and my heart already knows

[Pre-Chorus]
Everything I'm runnin' from is everything I'll miss
How's a man supposed to leave a whole life over this

[Chorus]
So I'm sittin' here at pump number two
Full tank, half a heart, and a town in the mirror I'm about to lose
That sign keeps buzzin' like it's beggin' me to stay
One light still on while I make up my mind to drive away
And I don't know if I'm brave or just a fool
Sittin' here at pump number two

[Verse 2]
Miss Donna waved goodbye like she already knew
Slid a coffee 'cross the counter, said "you'll figure it out, you always do"
The same stars I grew up under, the same crack in the lot
Everything I swore I'd leave is everything I've got

[Bridge]
Maybe one day I'll roll back in, dust on the plates
Pull up to this same old pump, older, but in one piece
'Til then the light'll be on, and the road will be long
And a piece of me stays parked right here, where I belong

[Final Chorus]
So I'm pullin' out of pump number two
Full tank, full eyes, and a prayer that I'm doin' what I gotta do
That sign gets smaller, but it never fades to gray
One light still on for the day I find my way
Back home, back to where I grew
Back to pump number two
```

# THE JUDGES' PANEL

*Five fictional industry archetypes, each scoring all 5 versions across five criteria
(**Hook**, **Singability/Radio**, **Emotion**, **Production Fit**, **Originality**), 10 points each — 50 max
per judge, 250 max overall.*

- **Rae Calhoun** — 25-year Music Row hit songwriter. Worships the hook and lyric economy.
- **Dwight Boone** — country radio program director. Asks one thing: "will it test in a truck?"
- **Hank Privett** — Texas dancehall traditionalist. Authenticity, instrumentation, can-you-two-step-to-it.
- **Mia Solano** — streaming/playlist curator & contemporary A&R. Vibe, freshness, replay value.
- **Loretta James** — touring artist & vocal coach. Lives for emotional delivery and the bridge.

## 🏆 Winner: **Version 1 — "Gas Station at Midnight"**

**Why it won:** it's the only version that scored top-3 with *every* judge — the rare song that the radio
PD, the songwriter, and the vocal coach all want for different reasons. It nails the exact brief: familiar,
emotional-but-not-depressing, a title that's in the hook, and a "fillin' up, breakin' down, leavin' town
or turnin' round" line that sticks after one listen. It doesn't win any single category outright — but it
loses none, and that balance is what makes a real single.

**The category crowns (so every version has a trophy):**
- 🎤 **Most emotional / best vocal moment:** V3 "Pump Number Two" — the panel's heart, runner-up overall.
- 📱 **Most current / best streaming play:** V4 "Neon Still On" — Mia's #1.
- 🤠 **Most authentic / best dancehall cut:** V5 "Open All Night" — Hank's #1, the two-step.
- 🎉 **Best live / tailgate singalong:** V2 "Last Light in Town" — the rowdy crowd-pleaser.

**Panel's closing note:** *"Cut V1 as the single, V3 as the tear-jerker track three, V4 for the playlists,
V5 for the bar, and play V2 last at the show when everybody's already singing."*
