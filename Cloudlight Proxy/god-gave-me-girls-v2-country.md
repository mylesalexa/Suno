# God Gave Me Girls — V2 (full arc, shorter)

*Tighter version of the girl-dad song that spans the whole journey — baby girl, to sixteen and her first
car (and first fender-bender, dad to the rescue), all the way to walking her down the aisle and giving her
away. Same hook, half the length, bigger emotional payoff. Male lead, heartfelt country.*

> **V2 vs V1:** shorter (pre-choruses trimmed, chorus tightened), and the timeline now runs cradle →
> first car / fender bender → wedding aisle. Keeps the "you never know what you're gonna get" framing
> (never says they wanted sons) and a Scripture nod (Psalm 139:14, fearfully and wonderfully made).

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal

**Genre:** heartfelt, warm country — radio-classic, sentimental, singable. Acoustic guitar, fiddle, pedal
steel, a little piano, light drums building under a big harmony chorus.

- **Tempo:** ~**98–104 BPM** — mid-tempo, warm; builds into the choruses, swells at the aisle.
- **Key:** **D major**, optional lift to **E** for the final chorus.
- **Vocal:** **male lead** — warm dad voice, a little grit, a lot of heart; conversational verses, full and
  emotional on the hook; let the bridge crack. Clean, no autotune.

## Full Lyrics

### Verse 1
You never know what you're gonna get — a fastball or a curve,
Then they laid her in my arms and I forgot every word.
Came the princess dresses, dance class, hairspray in the air,
A little glitter on this old man's heart, and I don't even care.

### Chorus
God gave me girls — ribbons and curls, the whole wide world in a twirl,
More feelin's than I ever knew, a love I'd give my life to prove.
And I wouldn't change a thing — turns out He knew me, He knew —
God gave me girls.

### Verse 2
Then I blinked and she's sixteen with the keys to her first car,
Prayin' on the porch every time those taillights got too far.
First fender bender, cryin' on the phone — "Daddy, what do I do?"
I was there in ten flat — Dad to the rescue.

### Chorus
God gave me girls — ribbons and curls, the whole wide world in a twirl,
More feelin's than I ever knew, a love I'd give my life to prove.

### Bridge
*(pull back, tender, building)*
Now I'm standin' at the back of a church in my Sunday tie,
That white dress takes my breath, and I'm tryin' hard not to cry.
Still fearfully and wonderfully made — Lord, when'd she grow? —
I'm givin' her away... but she's always gonna be my own.
*(soft, spoken)* "You ready, baby girl?" ... "Yeah, Daddy. Let's go."

### Final Chorus
*(big, key lift)*
God gave me girls — and one by one, I walked 'em down the aisle,
Handed 'em to good men with these daddy tears and a smile.
From that very first breath to the last "I do" —
God gave me girls... and He knew, He always knew.

### Outro
*(soft, slowing)*
Yeah... God gave me girls.
