# Headlights Off *(midnight on the water)*

**One-line concept:** A guy talks his girl into sneaking out after midnight, killing the headlights at the
end of the gravel, and slipping into the lake under a full moon — fireflies, warm water, nobody else awake.

**Strongest emotional angle:** the *thrill of a secret you only get to share with one person.* It's not
about the swim — it's that delicious, grinning, heart-pounding feeling of being young, in love, and
gloriously up to no good while the whole town sleeps. Warm, flirty, FUN — joy, not heartbreak.

---

## Why it's distinct (not a duplicate)
- **Nighttime, not daytime.** Lakeside is family/sun/turtles; this is moonlight, fireflies, headlights off,
  midnight swim. Opposite half of the clock.
- **The hook is an *action*, not a place.** "Headlights off" is a thing you *do* — instantly visual, easy to
  shout, and it doubles as "nobody can see us." That's the fresh angle the others don't own.
- **Skinny-dip handled as flirty-fun, not raunchy.** It's grins, dares, and a moon for a spotlight — PG-13
  and radio-safe, all tease and sparkle.

**Arc:** sneak the truck out (V1) → dare each other down the bank (pre) → kill the lights, moon takes over
(chorus) → fireflies + cold water + can't stop laughing (V2) → years from now we'll still sneak back (bridge)
→ biggest final chorus + chant.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** warm, mid-uptempo modern country with a flirty night-drive groove — breezy and conspiratorial in
the verses, big and open on the chorus. Acoustic + bright electric, slap-back twang, soft pedal steel for
the moonlight, claps and a four-on-the-floor pulse, gang-vocal hook.

- **Tempo:** ~**110 BPM** — relaxed but driving; let the final chorus bloom.
- **Key:** **D major**, lift to **E** for the final chorus.
- **Vocal:** **male lead** — warm, easygoing, a grin you can hear; conversational verses, wide-open chorus.
  Stack harmonies + claps on the hook and the "oh-oh" post-chorus. Clean, no autotune.
  *(Female-lead swap noted at the bottom.)*
