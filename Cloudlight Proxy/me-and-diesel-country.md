# Me and Diesel

*Female-vocal story country song — RaeLynn-style: bright, modern country-pop, sassy-but-warm, detail-rich
storytelling with a youthful twang and a big singable chorus. A rebel-hearted girl and Diesel — her
seventeen-hands-of-black gentle giant. He's big and slow, never fast, but loyal as the day is long. She
was terrified to climb up at first; learning to trot and post turned her fear into confidence and control,
and now they ride the open range together, inseparable. Told from her perspective.*

---

## Creative Direction

**Concept:** *The wild girl and the gentle giant — fear turned to trust.* The hook is the contrast: she's
a rebel who never did what she was told, but her soft spot is a big, slow, loyal horse named Diesel. The
emotional turn is universal and earned — the very thing that scared her (his size) became the thing that
gave her confidence, freedom, and a best friend.

**Why it works (RaeLynn DNA):**
- **Specific, true details** sell a country story — "seventeen hands of black," "learned to trot, learned
  to post," "stood there like a gentleman and waited out the scared." Real horsemanship language gives it
  authenticity and charm.
- **Character first.** She's drawn fast in V1 (came out kickin', tore through town like a brushfire), so
  the soft turn toward Diesel lands with personality, not sap.
- **The growth arc is the chorus payoff.** Scared → trot/post → control → inseparable. The listener roots
  for her, and the gentle-giant horse is impossible not to love.
- **Empowering + warm**, the RaeLynn sweet spot — a little sass, a big heart, sing-along chorus.

**Story arc:** wild girl meets the giant and steps back, scared (V1) → "you better not get on — but I
did" (pre) → who they are now: dust, open sky, slow-and-steady, loyal (chorus) → learning to ride, the
patient horse, gaining control (V2) → "the thing that scared me set me free" (pre 2) → the giant became
her steady heartbeat, fear into trust (bridge) → inseparable on the open range (final chorus).

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal

**Genre:** modern country-pop story song — bright, warm, radio-ready (RaeLynn lane). Acoustic guitar,
fiddle, banjo, pedal steel, light live drums, hand-claps optional; a big, singable, harmony-stacked
chorus. Heartfelt and a little sassy, not somber.

- **Tempo:** ~**110–116 BPM** — warm mid-tempo with a lift; rides like an easy canter.
- **Key:** **G major** (bright, hopeful), lift to **A** for the final chorus.
- **Vocal:** **female lead** — young, warm, twangy and expressive, conversational in the verses with
  personality, full and soaring in the chorus. Stack sweet harmonies on the hook. Clean, no autotune.

### Verse 1
Mama always said I came out kickin', never did a thing I'm told,
Tore through this town like a brushfire, sixteen and uncontrolled.
Then Daddy backed a trailer up with seventeen hands of black —
I took one look at that mountain and I took three steps back.

### Pre-Chorus
He was taller than the barn door, eyes like a comin' storm,

But I did.

### Chorus
Now it's me and Diesel, dust and open sky,
A thousand pounds of easy with a slow and steady stride.
He ain't gonna win no races, never cared to even try,
But he's the only one who never let me down or asked me why —
So let this wild girl ride,
Me and Diesel, side by side.

So I rode.

and he's mine.
