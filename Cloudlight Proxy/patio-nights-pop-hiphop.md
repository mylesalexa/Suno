# Patio Nights *(clean, chart-aimed summer pop/hip-hop)*

*A grown-and-cool — not kiddie, not provocative — summer pop-hip-hop banger about **patio nights**: golden
hour into midnight, string lights, good people, cold drinks, an easy smooth bounce. Clean (no profanity,
nothing explicit) but undeniably cool and radio-polished — built to top a summer playlist. Melodic sung-rap
verses, big sung hook. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief:** less family-themed, more chart-topping, not provocative. So it's *sophisticated clean* —
> adult summer-night vibe (toasts, string lights, good company), broad appeal, sing-along hook, smooth
> groove. Cool enough for the cookout AND the rooftop bar, clean enough for radio.

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the universal best night of summer that costs nothing — everyone on the patio, string lights
on, phones down, golden hour bleeding into a warm night, nobody in a hurry to leave. **Strongest angle:**
it's aspirational *and* relatable — that effortless, grown, good-vibes glow people screenshot and replay.
Clean by being classy, not childish: it's about the *feeling*, not the party excess.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** clean modern **pop / hip-hop / R&B-pop** summer groove — smooth bounce, warm chords, finger
snaps, mellow 808, lush vocal harmonies; **melodic sung-rap verses** into a big sung hook. Golden-hour,
radio-polished, sophisticated but catchy.

- **Tempo:** ~**100–106 BPM** — smooth head-nod bounce (not frantic).
- **Key:** **A minor** (cool, warm-night sophistication), bright lift on the hook.
- **Vocal:** smooth, confident **male** — melodic sung-rap on verses (tuneful, lots of pitch movement),
  full and warm on the chorus, stacked harmonies. Light auto-tune fine. Clean. (Female/duet swap noted.)

## ⭐ Best Suno prompt
```
Clean summer pop hip-hop, 104 BPM, key of A minor, smooth bounce, warm chords, finger snaps, mellow 808, melodic sung-rap verses, big sung hook, lush harmonies, golden-hour R&B-pop, radio polished
```
**Tip:** keep it **smooth, not hype** — head-nod bounce, snaps, warm chords; the cool comes from *restraint*.
"melodic sung-rap verses" keeps verses tuneful (not monotone). Clean stays clean via the Exclude list, but
the vibe reads *grown and sophisticated*, not safe. If it's too sleepy, nudge to 106; too stiff, add
`laid-back groove, summer night`.

## Variations
- **Female-lead / duet:** swap `male` → `smooth female vocal`, or `male-female trade-off` for a crossover duet.
- **More R&B:** add `neo-soul chords, Rhodes piano`. **More pop:** add `bright pop synths, radio chorus`.
