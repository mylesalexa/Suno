# Song of the Summer — 7 Contenders, New Judges, One Winner

*Seven country song-of-the-summer ideas, scored by a fresh "listen-on-repeat" panel, with the winner's
lyrics nailed down and Suno-ready. Targeted to 2026 country trends: groove + acoustic texture, flirty
windows-down warmth, and a hook that loops. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Trend read (Reddit/Billboard, mid-2026):** grooves and genre-blends are landing (Musgraves "Dry Spell"),
> acoustic guitar is back as a texture, the female-country wave is huge (Ella Langley, Megan Moroney), and
> "song of the summer" is a *cluster* — so the winner has to be the one you actually put on repeat. The
> criteria below weight exactly that.

---

## The Criteria (this panel only)
Each idea is scored on five things that make a **repeat-all-summer** song — 10 pts each, 50 per judge:
1. **Hook Loop** — does the chorus stick in your head after one listen?
2. **Replay Pull** — do you want it again *immediately* (low skip, high save)?
3. **Summer Vibe** — sun, freedom, windows-down, lake-and-tailgate feel.
4. **Chantability** — can a car full of people *and* a festival crowd scream it?
5. **Fresh/Viral** — a moment or angle that spreads and fits the 2026 sound.

## The 7 Contenders

**1. "Tan Lines"** *(flirty summer-fling groove, M or F lead)*
A summer romance you'll measure in tan lines when it's over — bittersweet flipped to *joyful*.
> Hook: *"By the time the summer unwinds, all I wanna be left with is the tan lines."*

**2. "Whole Lotta Summer"** *(big optimistic anthem, M)*
Pure windows-down euphoria — there's a whole lotta summer left and we're spending all of it.
> Hook: *"We got a whole lotta summer left to lose."*

**3. "Dirt Road Disco"** *(danceable genre-blend groove, M — the viral wildcard)*
Backroad tailgate with a pocket groove; the 2026 dance-country lane.
> Hook: *"Welcome to the dirt road disco, baby, kick it in the dust."*

**4. "Honeysuckle"** *(sweet Southern summer-love groove, F lead — rides the female wave)*
Slow-burn sensory groove; sweet, flirty, fresh.
> Hook: *"Kiss me slow like honeysuckle, drip a little summer on my tongue."*

**5. "Catch and Release"** *(clever fishing/fling double-meaning, M)*
A wink of a story song — summer love with no hooks set.
> Hook: *"This ain't love, baby, it's catch and release."*

**6. "Creek Water"** *(sunburnt freedom anthem, M or F)*
Baptized in the creek, sunburnt and alive — cheeky faith meets summer freedom.
> Hook: *"Creek water baptized, sunburnt and alive."*

**7. "Pink Lemonade"** *(sweet-but-spiked flirty bop, F lead)*
A girl (and a summer) that's sweet with a kick.
> Hook: *"Sweet as pink lemonade with a little kick of shine."*
