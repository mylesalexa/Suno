## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the "wedding social" — a Prairie-Canadian institution where the whole town rents the legion
hall, pays a small cover charge, and parties all night to help a couple cover their wedding costs. It's not
a wedding itself; it's the community fundraiser *before* the wedding, and it's often the rowdiest, most
beloved party of the year. **Why it works:** the details are hyper-specific and completely real (Texas
Mickey, toonies, 50/50 draw, meat draw, rye bread and garlic sausage at the midnight lunch) — that
specificity is what makes it feel like a genuine local anthem instead of a generic party song.

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre (full description, as provided):** modern country-pop dance — four-on-the-floor, gritty electric
guitar riff, banjo plucks over a club beat, slap bass, stomp-clap groove, punchy live drums with an 808
layer, confident male lead, tight harmony stacks, gang-vocal chorus, festival country swagger, polished
radio mix. Energetic, not novelty.

- **Tempo:** ~**128 BPM** (four-on-the-floor, club-meets-country energy).
- **Vocal:** confident **male lead** — tight harmony stacks, huge gang-vocal chorus.
