# The Good Stuff We Found

*A 90s-country family love song, written for a friend — female lead, slow-to-mid tempo ballad with
acoustic guitar, fiddle, and steel. Deep, heartfelt, sentimental, nostalgic: travel and family, late-night
red wine with her husband Mark, campfires and kids' laughter, the Disney castle, hiking and watching the
kids be brave. In the spirit of Michelle Wright, Tim McGraw, and George Strait — a 90s country road-trip
anthem that's really a love letter to an ordinary, beautiful life.*

> Her original lyrics, structured into song form and lightly tidied for flow/singability — all the names,
> places, and memories kept exactly. Nothing important changed; just shaped for Suno.

---

## Creative Direction

**Concept:** *"The good stuff" isn't the destinations — it's the people in them.* The verses travel the
world (old cities, mountain peaks, Disney), but every chorus comes home to the small sacred moments:
wine and stars on the back deck, kids' laughter in the pines, an evening walk with Mark. It's nostalgia
in the present tense — gratitude for a life while she's still living it.

**Why it lands:** the power is in the specifics — *Harper's laugh that could wake up the moon, Charley's
summer freckles, the castle in the distance.* 90s country lived on exactly this kind of plainspoken,
detail-rich heart (Strait's storytelling, McGraw's family warmth, Michelle Wright's earnest delivery).
Keeping the names makes it hers and unrepeatable.

**Arc:** the wanderer's suitcase, but "my favorite destination's by your side" (V1) → "the best parts
ain't the places on the trail" (pre) → the good stuff: wine, stars, campfire, kids (chorus) → the Disney
memory, kids again (V2) → the kids growing/being brave, time won't stand still (bridge) → home is Mark,
home is now (final chorus) → grateful, only just begun (outro).

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal

**Genre:** 90s country ballad — warm, organic, radio-classic. Acoustic guitar, fiddle, weeping pedal
steel, brushed drums, upright/electric bass; lush harmonies blooming on the choruses. Nothing modern or
processed — live-band, front-porch honesty.

- **Tempo:** ~**78–84 BPM** — slow-to-mid, a gentle sway; let it breathe.
- **Key:** **D major** (warm), optional lift to **E** for the final chorus.
- **Vocal:** **female lead** — warm, clear, sentimental, a touch of twang; conversational and tender in
  the verses, full and soaring on the hook. Sweet harmony stacks on "the good stuff we found." Clean, **no
  autotune** — 90s-organic.

## Full Lyrics

### Verse 1
Got a suitcase full of memories, a map with worn-out lines,
Chasing sunsets through old cities, making the most of borrowed time.
From cobblestone streets to mountain peaks, we've wandered far and wide,
But my favorite destination's always sitting by your side.

### Pre-Chorus
Every road keeps leading somewhere, every mile tells a tale,
But the best parts of this journey ain't the places on the trail.

### Chorus
It's red wine on the back deck, talking long after dark,
Just me and you and a million stars hanging over where we are.
It's campfire smoke and old friends' jokes, while the kids run wild and free,
Their laughter floating through the pines like a sweet old melody.
It's all these little moments that money just can't buy —
The good stuff we found along the ride.

### Verse 2
I still see those Disney lights shining bright against the night,
Walking down Main Street together, watching wonder fill their eyes.
That castle standing in the distance, like a dream we'd finally found,
And for a minute we were kids again, spinning circles all around.

### Chorus
It's red wine on the back deck, talking long after dark,
Just me and you and a million stars hanging over where we are.
It's campfire smoke and old friends' jokes, while the kids run wild and free,
Their laughter floating through the pines like a sweet old melody.
It's all these little moments that money just can't buy —
The good stuff we found along the ride.

### Bridge
*(soften, then build)*
Harper's got a laugh that could wake up the moon,
Comes rolling through the campsite like a favorite tune.
And Charley's summer freckles show up right on cue,
Like little drops of sunshine painted fresh and new.
I've watched them climb mountains that looked way too high,
Digging deep for courage with determination in their eyes.
And every step they take reminds me time won't stand still,
So I'm holding onto every memory I can fill.

### Final Chorus
It's evening walks with Mark, talking softly hand in hand,
Watching daylight fade away across this promised land.
It's passports, hiking trails, and all the places left to see,
But home is every moment when you're right here next to me.
Yeah, life's not in the headlines or the dreams we're chasing by —
It's the good stuff we found along the ride.

### Outro
*(soft, warm, ritardando)*
A world full of adventures, and we've only just begun,
Counting every blessing underneath the setting sun.
If tomorrow looks like today, I'll be satisfied —
With the good stuff we found along the ride.
