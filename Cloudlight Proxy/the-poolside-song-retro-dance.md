# The Poolside Song *(retro yacht-rock meets poolside hip-hop)*

*A campy, sax-forward summer goof in the lane of Saxboy Billy's viral "Puerto Rico Song / First Time in
San Juan" — "cheesy 1980s sitcom theme music mixed with yacht-rock," a strutting saxophone, a poolside
hip-hop groove, and gloriously **pointless, fun lyrics** (vibe over meaning, dance-routine friendly).
Clean. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Inspiration (not a copy):** same *vibe* as the viral AI sax travel songs — 80s sitcom-theme + yacht
> rock + big saxophone + goofy, meaningless-on-purpose lyrics that are stupidly catchy and danceable
> (Savannah-Bananas-style). All-original words; no artist names in the Suno boxes.

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the world's dumbest, happiest poolside anthem — sax solos, inflatable flamingos, cannonballs,
and lyrics that proudly mean *nothing*. **Strongest angle:** it leans all the way into "pointless" as the
joke (the bridge literally says *"doesn't mean a thing and that's the whole point"*), which is exactly why
that retro sax-goof formula goes viral — it's pure serotonin and a built-in dance.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** retro **80s yacht-rock meets poolside hip-hop / nu-disco** — a big strutting **saxophone lead**,
funky slap bass, four-on-the-floor with gated 80s drums, sunny chords, hand-claps; campy talk-sing/sing-rap
verses into a giant chant chorus with sax stabs. Cheesy-on-purpose, feel-good, very danceable.

- **Tempo:** ~**108–112 BPM** — strutting disco-funk bounce.
- **Key:** **C major** (bright, sunny).
- **Vocal:** fun, confident male — playful **sing-rap** on verses (lots of pitch movement, never monotone),
  big goofy chant on the chorus. Gang shouts + call-and-response. Light auto-tune fine. Clean.
