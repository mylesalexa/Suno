# The Poolside Song *(retro yacht-rock meets poolside hip-hop)*

*A sunny, sax-forward summer groove in the lane of Saxboy Billy's viral "Puerto Rico Song" — but where
that song earnestly *explains Puerto Rico*, this one earnestly **explains poolside life**: the slippery
deck, the skimmer, the chlorine, the filter, walk-don't-run. True little details spun warm and singable
over an 80s yacht-rock / poolside-hip-hop groove with a strutting saxophone. Clean. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **The angle (corrected):** *not* dumb/nonsense lyrics — **descriptive, charming, true poolside facts**
> sung earnestly and catchy (that's exactly why the San Juan song works: simple real observations,
> delivered with a straight face over a fun retro beat). Original words; no artist names in the Suno boxes.

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** a friendly tour of *poolside life* set to a retro sax groove — how you keep a pool, how you
act around it, and how good it feels to just be there. **Strongest angle:** the charm is sincerity —
singing genuinely useful, specific pool details ("the skimmer pulls the junk," "walk, don't run, it's
slippery when wet") makes it both funny-because-true *and* a legit feel-good summer bop you can dance to.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** retro **80s yacht-rock meets poolside hip-hop / nu-disco** — a strutting **saxophone lead**,
funky slap bass, four-on-the-floor with gated 80s drums, sunny chords, hand-claps; warm, earnest **sung**
melodic verses into a big catchy chorus with sax stabs. Cheesy-good, feel-good, danceable.

- **Tempo:** ~**108–112 BPM** — strutting disco-funk bounce.
- **Key:** **C major** (bright, sunny).
- **Vocal:** warm, confident male — **sung and melodic**, earnest delivery (sells the simple facts), big
  on the chorus. A little sing-rap lilt is fine; light auto-tune optional. Clean.
