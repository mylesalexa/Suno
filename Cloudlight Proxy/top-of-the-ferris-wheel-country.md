# Top of the Ferris Wheel *(last night of the fair)*

*A county-fair, summer-night young-love song. Boy meets girl on the last night of the fair, talks her onto
the Ferris wheel, and steals a first kiss right when it stops at the top — midway lights below, neon in her
eyes, cotton candy and funnel-cake air. Nostalgic, warm, romantic, joyful — a big sing-along you loop on
repeat. Warm male lead. Cloudlight Proxy.*

**Strongest emotional angle:** the universal, time-stopping magic of a *first kiss at the very top of the
Ferris wheel* on the one night that can't last — small-town, everybody's-been-there nostalgia that feels
like the best night of your life happening in real time. Sweet, not sad: the fair ends, the feeling doesn't.

---

## The arc (why it loops)
- **Verse 1:** the midway at dusk — lights flick on, ring toss, funnel cake, he spots her in the crowd.
- **Pre:** he spends his last few dollars to win the bear, just to walk her to the line.
- **Chorus:** the payoff hook — the wheel stops at the top, town spread out below, first kiss, neon in her eyes.
- **Post-Chorus:** a tiny chantable loop ("round and round") built for short-form.
- **Verse 2:** the wheel comes down but the night keeps going — shared cotton candy, names carved, slow walk.
- **Bridge:** years later he still smells funnel cake and he's right back up there with her.
- **Final Chorus:** biggest, key lift — last night of the fair, but he never came back down.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** modern commercial country — bright, nostalgic, feel-good. Acoustic-driven verses, full band on
the choruses, banjo roll and fiddle for the carnival sparkle, hand-claps and a big chantable hook.

- **Tempo:** ~**116 BPM** — buoyant mid-uptempo; let the final chorus open up biggest.
- **Key:** **G major**, lift to **A** for the final chorus.
- **Vocal:** **male lead** — warm, youthful, a grin in the verses turning tender at the top of the wheel.
  Stacked harmonies + claps on the hook. Clean, no autotune. *(Female-lead swap noted at the bottom.)*
