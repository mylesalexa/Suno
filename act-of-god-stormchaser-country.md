# Act of God

*Driving, moody modern country — the storm-chaser's prayer (a companion / V2 to "One Eye on the Sky").
Same chaser, but this time the storm turns biblical: hail hammering the truck like thrown stone, lightning
walking the field, the whole thing a literal **act of God** — and he's begging that same God to get him
out alive. Adrenaline and fear curdle into a foxhole prayer. Polished modern-country anthem voice
(matching the catalog), dark and atmospheric, built to hit fast.*

> **Original — not a copy.** No artist names in the Suno box. Shares the "one eye on the road, one eye on
> the sky" thread with its companion track but stands on its own.

---

## Creative Direction

**Concept:** *Caught in the teeth of the storm, the chaser starts praying.* The thrill of the first song
gives way to awe and terror — hail, lightning, the sheer power of it — and a desperate, honest prayer for
protection. The hook plays on "act of God" (the insurance phrase *and* the literal truth): *they call it
an act of God — well God, then this is You, so get me through.*

**Why it grips:**
- **Visceral, escalating danger:** hail going from golf balls to baseballs, lightning "lookin' for my
  name," a bolt so close "I felt it in my teeth." You feel the windshield shake.
- **The foxhole prayer is real, not preachy:** "I don't deserve a rescue, chasin' down the storm — but
  I'm callin' on Your name." Bargaining, fear, and awe — how people actually pray when it's bad.
- **The wordplay carries weight:** "act of God" flips from a claims-adjuster term to a man literally
  face-to-face with the power of God.
- **Smallness + closeness:** "never felt so small, never felt You quite so close" — terror and reverence
  in the same breath.

**Authentic detail (researched):** the sudden temperature drop and darkening sky, hail that grows with the
storm's energy, close lightning strikes, the truck taking a beating — grounded so the fear reads real.

**Emotional arc:** the sky turns and the first hail hits (V1) → lightning hunting him, he starts to pray
(pre) → the prayer-hook: act of God, have mercy, get me out alive (chorus) → the near-strike, "let there
be a tomorrow to quit" (V2) → "I don't deserve a rescue, but I'm callin' Your name" (pre 2) → the awe/bargain
bridge: never felt so small, hold me through the night (bridge) → final chorus → "get me home alive."

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal

**Genre:** driving, *moody* modern country — radio/arena country, *not* rock or grunge. Acoustic guitar,
dark brooding electric, pedal steel, and fiddle over a fast, punchy country beat. Opens into a big,
dramatic chorus with tight harmonies. Shadowy and reverent, not sunny. (No banjo — keep it dark.)

- **Tempo:** ~**150–154 BPM** — hard-driving, high-octane, with weight; **hit the first verse fast.**
- **Key:** **E minor** — dark and dramatic, the chorus opens big but stays heavy, not bright.
- **Vocal:** warm, powerful, *polished* modern-country male lead — clean and strong, big and emotive (the
  same radio-country anthem voice as "Everybody Knows Somebody" / "Where We Belong," **not** gritty or
  grungy). Urgent and steady in the verses; the chorus prayer should sound desperate but full, never barked.

## Full Lyrics

### Verse 1
Sky went black at noon and the temperature dropped ten,
First stone cracked the windshield like the Lord cocked back again.
Golf balls turnin' baseballs, beatin' dents into the hood,
And I'm drivin' straight toward the very thing I know I never should.

### Pre-Chorus
Lightnin' walked across the field like it was lookin' for my name —
So I grip the wheel and whisper through the thunder and the rain —

### Chorus
Lord, they call it an act of God — well, God, then this is You,
So if it's Your hand swingin', I'm beggin' it pulls me through.
Hail on the roof like a hammer, lightnin' splittin' the dark,
One eye on the road and one hand on my heart —
I ain't ready, I ain't done, don't let it be my time —
Act of God, have mercy — get me out alive.

### Verse 2
Bolt came down so close I felt it rattle in my teeth,
Lit the whole world white and put the fear of God in me.
Hailstones in the ditch the size of a closed-up fist,
And I swear I'll quit tomorrow — Lord, just let there be a tomorrow to quit.

### Pre-Chorus
I don't deserve a rescue, chasin' down the storm —
But I'm callin' on Your name like I ain't done since I was born —

### Chorus
Lord, they call it an act of God — well, God, then this is You,
So if it's Your hand swingin', I'm beggin' it pulls me through.
Hail on the roof like a hammer, lightnin' splittin' the dark,
One eye on the road and one hand on my heart —
I ain't ready, I ain't done, don't let it be my time —
Act of God, have mercy — get me out alive.

### Bridge
*(drops down, intimate, building)*
I have never felt so small, never felt You quite so close,
Out here where the only thing between me and the grave is hope.
If You spare me one more sunrise, Lord, I swear I'll make it right —
But right now just hold me in Your hand and get me through the night —
Here it comes — cover me —

### Final Chorus
*(bigger, desperate)*
Lord, they call it an act of God — well, God, then this is You,
So if it's Your hand swingin', I'm beggin' it pulls me through.
Hail on the roof like a hammer, lightnin' splittin' the dark,
One eye on the road and one hand on my heart —
I ain't ready, I ain't done, don't let it be my time —
Act of God, have mercy — get me out alive.

### Outro
*(driving off, fading)*
One eye on the road, one eye on the sky —
Act of God, have mercy... get me home alive.
