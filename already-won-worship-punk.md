# Already Won

*Christian worship pop-punk — THE CLOSER. The sunrise the whole record earns. After nine songs of storm,
fear, deception, rescue, surrender, and endurance, this is the victory lap: death is defeated, He's
coming back, every tear gets wiped away — the war is already won. The biggest, fastest, most joyful track
on the album, and its outro reprises the earlier hooks so the entire record comes home in one final shout.*

---

## Creative Direction

**Topic:** *The victory is already won — resurrection, the return, and the end of fear.* The album has
lived in tension; this song cashes it in. It's end-times awareness flipped fully to hope: not "hold on,
it's scary," but "hold on, we already know how it ends." Defeat-proof joy.

**Why it's the closer (and how it ties everything together):**
- It **resolves every thread**: the chaos of "Anchor" ends, the unknown future of "I Don't Know" is
  revealed as glory, the deceived/falling-away world of "Ever Learning" is answered by the One who comes
  back to make it right, and the redeemed of "Empty"/"Under"/"Off the Fence" finally come home.
- The **bridge is the album bow** — it reprises the record's own hooks ("holding on to You," "I came up
  new," "lover of the Light," "Truth has a name") and lands on *"Come, Lord Jesus."* Everything comes home.
- It sends the listener out on the **highest, most hopeful note** — exactly where a record like this should end.

**Bible verses:**
1. **1 Corinthians 15:55, 57** — "Where, O death, is your victory? Where, O death, is your sting?... thanks be to God! He gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ." *(the hook)*
2. **Romans 8:37** — "in all these things we are more than conquerors through him who loved us." *(the chorus)*
3. **Revelation 21:4** — "He will wipe every tear from their eyes. There will be no more death." *(verse 2)*
4. **Revelation 1:18** — "I hold the keys of death and Hades." *(verse 2)*
5. **John 16:33** — "Take heart! I have overcome the world." *(callback to "Anchor")*
6. **Romans 8:38–39** — "neither death nor life... will be able to separate us from the love of God." *(pre-chorus 2)*
7. **1 Thessalonians 4:16** — "the Lord himself will come down... with the trumpet call of God." *(verse 2 — the return)*
8. **Revelation 22:20** — "Amen. Come, Lord Jesus." *(the bridge — Maranatha)*

**Emotional arc:** they say the dark wins — but the stone already rolled away (V1) → "go ahead and shake,
I read the final page" (pre) → the explosion: death, where's your sting, He already won (chorus) → the
return, the keys of death, every tear wiped (V2) → "nothing can separate us" (pre 2) → the bow: reprise
of the album's hooks + "Come, Lord Jesus" (bridge) → biggest final chorus → triumphant outro shout.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal

**Genre:** triumphant, celebratory pop-punk — the fastest and biggest on the record. Wall of driving
distorted guitars, soaring octave leads, relentless punk drums, huge layered gang vocals, a final key
change. Pure joy with weight behind it.

- **Tempo:** ~**170–176 BPM** — fast, soaring, celebratory.
- **Key:** **E** major, lift to **F#** for the final chorus.
- **Vocal:** full-throated, jubilant male lead — big and open everywhere, never restrained. Maximum gang
  vocals on the "already won" hooks and the reprise bridge — the whole band/crowd at full volume.
