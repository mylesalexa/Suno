# Still (Be Still)

*Christian worship pop-punk — toned-down companion to "Anchor." Good Charlotte energy softened into
Boys Like Girls melodic emo-pop-rock: chiming clean-guitar verses, big emotional sung choruses, hopeful
and anchored in Christ. Where "Anchor" white-knuckles and holds on, this one exhales and gets still.*

---

## Creative Direction

**Topic:** *Peace in the chaos — "Be still, and know that I am God."* The companion truth to "Anchor."
Instead of gripping tight against the shaking, this song answers the chaos by **letting go and getting
still**, because Jesus is the calm we can't manufacture. It's the quieter, more vulnerable, more melodic
side of the same faith-over-fear coin — which is exactly the Good-Charlotte-meets-Boys-Like-Girls tonal
shift: less shout, more soar.

**Why it works:** the emo-pop-rock lane runs on late-night honesty and big emotional release. Naming the
3 a.m. anxiety/doom-scroll first makes the "be still" payoff land as *relief*, not a platitude. It stays
hopeful and Christ-centered without going doom-and-gloom, and the "Be still" hook is instantly singable.

**Bible verses:**
1. **Psalm 46:10** — "Be still, and know that I am God." *(the hook)*
2. **Mark 4:39** — Jesus rebukes the storm: "Peace! Be still!" and it was completely calm. *(the bridge)*
3. **John 14:27** — "Peace I leave with you... Do not let your hearts be troubled and do not be afraid."
4. **Philippians 4:6–7** — "Do not be anxious... the peace of God, which transcends all understanding, will guard your hearts."
5. **Matthew 11:28–29** — "Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest." *(verse 2)*
6. **Isaiah 26:3** — "You will keep in perfect peace those whose minds are steadfast, because they trust in you."
7. **Psalm 23:2–3** — "He leads me beside quiet waters, he refreshes my soul."

**Emotional arc:** restless, anxious verse (3 a.m., the noise) → pre-chorus surrender ("done pretending
I'm fine") → soaring chorus of release ("I'll be still") → grounded verse 2 (the invitation: come to Me)
→ bridge worship peak ("Peace, be still" — the One in the boat) → bigger final chorus → tender sung outro.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal

**Genre:** melodic emo pop-punk / pop-rock worship — Good Charlotte drive softened with Boys Like Girls
melody. Clean chiming/arpeggiated guitar verses, big distorted but *melodic* choruses, live punk drums
pulled back a notch, octave-stacked "oh-oh" harmonies (sung, not shouted), one soaring lead guitar line.

- **Tempo:** ~**140–148 BPM** (mid-tempo — driving but it breathes; slower than "Anchor's" 168).
- **Key:** **G** major, lift to **A** for the final chorus.
- **Vocal:** young, emotional, melodic male lead — earnest and a little aching in the verses, full and
  soaring in the chorus. Harmonies stacked and *sung* on the hook rather than gang-shouted; save the big
  group vocal for the very last chorus and outro only.
