# Empty (All I Need)

*Christian worship pop-punk — the heart-gripper deep cut. Not an anthem: a testimony. Chased every vice
to the bottom, came up empty, got found by grace, and now Jesus is the only thing that fills the hole.
Dynamic and raw — intimate verses that build to a cathartic, gut-punch chorus. Still punk, but it bleeds.*

---

## Creative Direction

**Topic:** *Redemption from addiction/vice — "I chased everything and it left me empty; then Jesus found
me, and now He's all I need."* A first-person testimony, not a sermon. The power is in the honesty: it
doesn't clean up the wreckage before the grace shows up.

**Why it works:** the EP needed a track that grips the heart instead of raising a fist. Specificity sells
it — the bottle, the empty highs, the kingdom built on nothing — and the turn (grace meeting you at your
worst, *before* you fix yourself) is the most powerful idea in the gospel. The dynamic build (whispered
bottom → full-band catharsis) mirrors the story: empty, then overflowing.

**Bible verses:**
1. **John 4:13–14** — "Everyone who drinks this water will be thirsty again, but whoever drinks the water I give will never thirst." *(the chorus — living water)*
2. **Luke 15:20** — "While he was still a long way off... his father ran to him." *(the prodigal — "You just ran")*
3. **Psalm 40:2** — "He lifted me out of the slimy pit... and set my feet on a rock." *(the pre-chorus / bridge)*
4. **2 Corinthians 5:17** — "If anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: the old has gone." *(verse 2)*
5. **Psalm 23:1, 5** — "The Lord is my shepherd, I lack nothing... my cup overflows." *(the "all I need" / outro)*
6. **2 Corinthians 12:9** — "My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness." *(the bridge)*
7. **Isaiah 55:2** — "Why spend money on what does not satisfy?" *(verse 1 — the empty chase)*

**Emotional arc:** the chase and the emptiness (V1) → hitting bottom, the cry (pre-chorus) → grace runs
to him, empty becomes overflowing (chorus) → the change, old self dead, new life (V2) → the confession
that lands the whole song: *"I'm not standing 'cause I'm strong — His grace is enough"* (bridge) → full
cathartic final chorus → bare, intimate outro: *"I came in empty, now my cup runs over."*

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal

**Genre:** raw, dynamic pop-punk — emotional deep cut, not anthem. Intimate clean/arpeggiated guitar in
the verses (bass and ghosted drums underneath), exploding into a driving, distorted, *cathartic* chorus.
Big dynamic range is the whole point. Minimal gang vocals — this one rides a single raw lead.

- **Tempo:** ~**144–148 BPM**, with real dynamic dips (verses pulled way back, chorus full).
- **Key:** **D** major, with **minor-leaning verses** (B minor feel) resolving up into the chorus — that
  shadow-to-light shift carries the testimony.
- **Vocal:** gritty, vulnerable, *cracking* male lead — near-spoken and quiet at the bottom of the verses,
  building to full-throated and a little broken in the chorus. Let it sound real, not polished. Save any
  backing/group vocal for soft echoes on the outro ("all I need").
