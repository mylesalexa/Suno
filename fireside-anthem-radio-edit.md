# Long Weekend (Light It Up)

*Canadian campfire / long-weekend anthem — radio edit, V2*

> The creative brief: what does a Canadian campfire night actually *feel* like? It's the
> two-hour drive up the 400 with the windows down. It's the screen door slapping shut. It's
> a loon out on black water, mosquitoes you stopped swatting three beers ago, somebody's
> uncle's guitar with a string out of tune, the fish that doubles in size every time it's
> told, and the quiet drive home Monday already missing it. This version chases that — tighter,
> hookier, built for a car stereo and a crowd that doesn't know the words yet but will by the
> second chorus.

---

## Finished Lyric Draft

### Verse 1
Two-hour drive and the radio's gone,
Just the wind and a half-remembered song.
Screen door slaps, down the path that's worn,
Dock's still warm, beat the comin' storm —
Loon on the lake like she's callin' us home,
Phones in a drawer 'cause there's no service zone —
Crack the first one, hear it hiss,
Man, the whole damn year was waitin' for this.

### Pre-Chorus
And the sun drops low and gold,
Somebody hands me a chair and a story half-told —
*This* is the part they don't put on the postcards, no,
The best night of the summer that nobody's gonna know.

### Chorus
So light it up, light it up, watch it climb to the stars!
We got a two-four, a campfire, and a beat-up guitar.
Sing it loud for the long weekend, throw your arms in the air —
**(Whoa-oh-oh-oh)** for the best ones who got here,
**(Whoa-oh-oh-oh)** light it up, I don't care —
This is the night, this is the night, this is the night!

### Verse 2
The stories grow taller the lower the sun,
And the fish gets bigger with every one —
There's a laugh by the fire so big you can't hardly stand,
And a six-string gettin' passed from hand to hand.
Northern lights or a trick of the smoke?
Out here you don't question, you just let it go —
We're a tangle of plaid and a chorus of fools,
And I swear these are the people I choose.

### Pre-Chorus
'Cause the sun's gone down for good,
And the firelight's doin' what the daylight never could —
*This* is the part you don't see comin', no,
The best night of your life and you're too in it to know.

### Chorus
So light it up, light it up, watch it climb to the stars!
We got a two-four, a campfire, and a beat-up guitar.
Sing it loud for the long weekend, throw your arms in the air —
**(Whoa-oh-oh-oh)** for the best ones who got here,
**(Whoa-oh-oh-oh)** light it up, I don't care —
This is the night, this is the night, this is the night!

### Bridge

One day the cottage gets sold,
The drive feels longer, the summers feel old —
But somewhere a kid throws a log on the flame,
And a loon calls back, and it's all the same...
So while we got it — *(build)* —
While the fire's still high — *(louder)* —
While the best of 'em are here tonight —
**(everybody now!)**

### Final Double Chorus
Light it up, light it up, watch it climb to the stars!
We got a two-four, a campfire, and a beat-up guitar.
Sing it loud for the long weekend, throw your arms in the air —
**(Whoa-oh-oh-oh)** for the best ones who got here,
**(Whoa-oh-oh-oh)** light it up, I don't care —

*(key lift / full crowd)*
Light it up, LIGHT IT UP, 'til the sun hits the bay!
One more song 'fore the Monday comes and takes us away —
Sing it loud for the long weekend, every voice that I love —
**(Whoa-oh-oh-oh)** raise 'em up, raise 'em up,
**(Whoa-oh-oh-oh)** to the best ones we got —
This is the night, this is the night, this is the night!

### Outro Chant
*(fading, claps, the fire dying down)*
Whoa-oh-oh-oh... (light it up)
Whoa-oh-oh-oh... (sing along)
Whoa-oh-oh-oh...
...best night of our lives.

## Suno Music Prompt (short)

> Radio-ready Canadian folk-rock anthem, big singalong long-weekend energy. Driving acoustic guitar, fiddle, mandolin, anthemic kick-drum stomp, hand claps, huge gang-vocal "whoa-oh-oh" hooks. Warm and rowdy and nostalgic, raw at the edges but built for radio. Male lead, wide crowd harmonies on the chorus, key change in the final chorus. Like a campfire singalong that fills a stadium.

---

## Recommended Genre / Style
Anthemic Canadian folk-rock / roots-pop crossover — the radio-friendly cousin of a kitchen-party singalong. Lineage of Great Big Sea and The Tragically Hip pushed toward Mumford-style stomp-and-holler arena-folk so it reads on a car stereo. Acoustic guitar + fiddle + mandolin core, four-on-the-floor stomp, claps, gang vocals, optional B3/accordion swell under the last chorus.

## Tempo & Vocal Feel
- **~126–132 BPM**, 4/4, hard stomp on 1 and 3, claps on 2 and 4.
- **Key:** D major in the verses, lift to **E or F** for the final chorus.
- **Vocal:** Warm, slightly rasped male lead — easy and conversational in verses, full-throated open vowels in the chorus. The "whoa-oh-oh" is the radio hook: layered, slightly imperfect crowd vocals, the kind a bar full of strangers can nail on the first listen. Bridge strips to one voice + claps, then explodes.

## Alternate Chorus Hook Options
1. **"Light it up, light it up, watch it climb to the stars"** *(primary radio hook)*
2. **"It's a two-four, a campfire, and a beat-up guitar / and the best ones I know under the same set of stars"**
3. **"Whoa-oh, long weekend — never want it to end"**
4. **"Throw another year on the fire, let it burn / one more long weekend, one more song, one more turn"**
5. **"This is the night, this is the night, this is the night"** *(works as a stadium call-back)*
6. **"Sing it loud for the lake and the late nights / for the best ones, the firelight, the best of our lives"**

---

*Suno tuning:* tag **(gang vocals)**, **(claps)**, **(stomp)**, **(crowd shout)** on the choruses; **(solo voice, claps only)** at the top of the bridge then **(full band, key change)** on the final chorus. If the "whoa-oh-oh" comes out thin, add **(layered crowd oohs, anthemic)**.
