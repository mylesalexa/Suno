# God Gave Me Girls

*A heartfelt girl-dad country song — male lead, warm and a little funny, building to a big sentimental
singalong. The frame: you never know what you're gonna get (blue or pink, a fastball or a curve), and God
gave him daughters — and it couldn't be better. Packs in the whole girl-dad world (princess dresses, dance
class, shoes, hairspray, curling irons, more emotions than he's used to), and crowns them as beautiful as
their momma — the ultimate beauty — with Scripture woven through.*

> **Important framing (per request):** the song never says they *wanted* sons. It only celebrates the
> not-knowing ("blue or pink, a fastball or a curve, who knows") and lands on the joy of girls being
> exactly God's plan.

---

## Creative Direction

**Concept:** *God's plan beat my plan — He gave me girls, and I wouldn't trade it for the world.* Built in
the dad-daughter country tradition (Tim McGraw "My Little Girl," "I Loved Her First") — unconditional
love, pride, a tough guy gone soft — but celebratory and present-tense rather than a wedding farewell.

**Why it works:**
- **The "you never know" frame** is universal and gracious — it makes the gratitude land without ever
  hinting at disappointment.
- **The girl-dad list is fun and true** (glitter in the truck, loudest dad at the recital, crying at
  cartoons) — specificity = charm and singability.
- **The bridge gives it weight and faith** — momma as the ultimate beauty, the girls growing into her
  image, and Scripture spoken over them (worth beyond looks, strength, God within them).

**Scripture woven in (bridge):**
- **Proverbs 31:30** — "Charm is deceptive, and beauty is fleeting" → *"charm can fade and beauty's fleeting, that's what the Good Book says."*
- **Psalm 139:14** — "fearfully and wonderfully made" → *"you're fearfully and wonderfully made."*
- **Proverbs 31:25** — "clothed with strength and dignity" → *"clothed in strength and dignity."*
- **Psalm 46:5** — "God is within her, she will not fall" → *"God is right there within you... you will never break."*
- *(Psalm 127:3 — children are a heritage/reward from the Lord — the song's underlying heart.)*

**Arc:** the nine-month not-knowing, then she's in his arms (V1) → "I thought I had it planned, heaven
laughed" (pre) → God gave me girls, wouldn't trade a thing (chorus) → the girl-dad list, gone soft (V2) →
momma's beauty + Scripture spoken over them (bridge) → it couldn't be better (final chorus) → tender outro.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal

**Genre:** heartfelt, warm country — radio-classic, sentimental but easy to sing. Acoustic guitar, fiddle,
pedal steel, a little piano, light drums building under a big harmony chorus. Tender, joyful, organic.

- **Tempo:** ~**96–104 BPM** — mid-tempo, warm sway; builds into the choruses.
- **Key:** **D major**, optional lift to **E** for the final chorus.
- **Vocal:** **male lead** — warm, easygoing dad voice with a little grit and a lot of heart;
  conversational in the verses, full and emotional on the hook; let the bridge get tender. Clean, no autotune.

## Full Lyrics

### Verse 1
You spend nine whole months a-wonderin', is it gonna be blue or pink,
Paintin' little pictures in your head of who that soul might be.
'Cause you never know what's comin' — a fastball or a curve —
Then they laid her in my arms, and Lord, I lost every other word.

### Pre-Chorus
I thought I had my whole life planned — but heaven had to laugh,
'Cause He knew the very thing this heart was missin' all along —

### Chorus
So God gave me girls — ribbons and curls, the whole wide world in a twirl,
Tiny shoes and tea for two, more feelin's than I ever knew.
And I would not trade a thing — not one tear, not one princess dream —
Turns out He knew me better than I did — so God gave me girls.

### Verse 2
Now there's glitter in my truck seats and the bathroom's always a wreck,
Curlin' irons, cans of hairspray, sparkles on a Sunday dress.
I'm front row at the dance class, I'm the loudest dad in town,
Cheerin' through the whole recital with my heart all over the ground.

### Pre-Chorus
I used to be all hard edges — now I cry at cartoon shows,
Funny how these little girls done rearranged a grown man's soul —

### Chorus
So God gave me girls — ribbons and curls, the whole wide world in a twirl,
Tiny shoes and tea for two, more feelin's than I ever knew.
And I would not trade a thing — not one tear, not one princess dream —
Turns out He knew me better than I did — so God gave me girls.

### Bridge
*(pull back, tender, building)*
And they're growin' up the image of the woman that I married,
The prettiest thing these eyes have seen — and I'll tell 'em every day:
Charm can fade and beauty's fleeting, that's what the Good Book says,
So you're fearfully and wonderfully made, my daughters of the King.
Clothed in strength and dignity, and when the whole world shakes,
God is right there within you, girls — and you will never break —

### Final Chorus
*(big, warm, key lift)*
'Cause God gave me girls — ribbons and curls, the whole wide world in a twirl,
Tiny shoes and tea for two, more feelin's than I ever knew.
And it couldn't be better, no — this couldn't be —
Heaven looked down, knew just what I'd need — so God gave me girls.

### Outro
*(soft, warm, slowing)*
Yeah, God gave me girls...
And it couldn't be better —
God gave me girls.
