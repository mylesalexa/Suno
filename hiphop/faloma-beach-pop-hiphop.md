# Faloma Nights *(clean hip-hop dance-party banger — beach bonfire, late-night)*

*The night-party rewrite of the Faloma cut: clean hip-hop **dance-party** banger about **Faloma Beach after
dark** — bonfire on the sand, speakers in the sand, dancing under the stars on a hidden Whiteshell bay,
bass in your chest, hands in the sky 'til sunrise. 128 BPM, hard 808, club synths, real drops. Move-and-groove
energy, clean but grown and cool. Melodic sung-rap verses into a huge party hook. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief / the change:** the daytime cut was still too slow. This is the **hip-hop dance-party** version —
> fast, hard 808, four-on-the-floor club groove, drops and a dance break, all about the **late-night beach
> bonfire**. Kept the niche Faloma/Whiteshell setting (hidden bay off the 301, granite cliffs, loons) but flipped it to night.

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the secret beach turns into the best party in the Whiteshell once the sun goes down — bonfire,
bass, sand underfoot, stars overhead, no neighbors for miles so you can be as loud as you want. **Strongest
angle:** the *hidden* spot is the flex — "off the 301 where they never gonna find us" = a private after-dark
rave on a Manitoba lake. Clean by being **cool and grown** (it's about the groove and the night, not excess).

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** clean **hip-hop dance-party** banger — driving **four-on-the-floor**, hard 808 bass, club synths,
claps, party gang-chants; **melodic sung-rap verses** into a huge sung hook with a real **EDM drop** and a
dance break. Late-night beach-bonfire energy, hands-up, radio-and-club polished.

- **Tempo:** ~**128 BPM** — full club dance tempo (bumped hard from the 110/118 cuts).
- **Key:** **A minor** (night-time club energy, bright euphoric lift on the drop).
- **Vocal:** confident **male** — melodic sung-rap on verses (tuneful, hyped, lots of pitch movement), big
  and euphoric on the hook, gang-vocal chants. Light auto-tune fine. Clean. (Female/duet swap noted.)
