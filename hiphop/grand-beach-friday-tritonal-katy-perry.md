# Grand Beach Friday *(Tritonal × Katy Perry "Last Friday Night" — festival dance-pop, no Latin)*

*A **new** Grand Beach beach-party anthem — **not** the Latin/"Hips Don't Lie" cut. This one fuses **Katy
Perry's "Last Friday Night (T.G.I.F.)"** (playful party-recap pop, new-wave-flavored synth stabs, a **strummy
guitar riff hook**, oh-oh gang chants, "do it all again" energy) with the **Tritonal** half (euphoric festival
future-bass drop, soaring supersaws, lush emotive chords, sidechain pump). No trumpets, no Spanish, no sax —
guitar riff + synth stabs + supersaws instead. Dancing in the dark on the white dunes of Lake Winnipeg, a
driftwood bonfire, sand as soft as silk. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief:** a version that's **not Latin themed** — a fusion of **Tritonal** and **Katy Perry "Last Friday
> Night"** — as its own new file. Fresh lyrics (a Friday-night party story, not a Faloma reskin), still set at
> Grand Beach with the required beats: dancing in the dark, gathering driftwood to burn, sand as soft as silk.
> **No saxophone** — swapped for a strummy guitar riff + synth stabs (the other real "Last Friday Night" 80s touches).

## The fusion recipe (Katy Perry × Tritonal)
- **Verses = Katy Perry "Last Friday Night":** playful, bouncy **story-pop** (we drove out, kicked off our
  shoes, strung the lights), **zigzagging synth stabs**, a bright **strummy guitar riff** hook, claps,
  "T.G.I.F." attitude.
- **Drop = Tritonal:** white-noise riser → **soaring supersaw leads + lush emotive chords + sidechain pump**,
  a euphoric hands-up festival drop — with the **guitar riff riding on top of the supersaws** (the weld that
  keeps it fun *and* huge).
- **128 BPM**, four-on-the-floor. **A minor** so the drop lands euphoric (Tritonal) while the verses stay bright and playful (Katy Perry).
- **No Latin, no sax:** trumpets/congas/dembow/Spanish/saxophone are out — the guitar riff is the party hook, the supersaws are the sky.

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the classic Katy Perry **"what a night" party recap**, relocated to Grand Beach and blown up with
a Tritonal festival drop. Verses tell the story with a wink (windows down, string lights, dancing on the
picnic table, no signal); the chorus **detonates** into euphoric supersaws so *"dancin' in the dark"* becomes
a festival moment. The **"do it all again"** outro is the Last-Friday-Night signature.
**How it's different from the other two:** *Faloma* = Latin×Tritonal (trumpet); *Grand Beach Nights (Latin)* =
same lane for Grand Beach; **this** = Katy-Perry party-pop × Tritonal, guitar riff not trumpet or sax, brand-new lyrics.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **festival dance-pop** — Katy Perry "Last Friday Night" playfulness (zigzagging synth stabs, a
strummy guitar riff hook, handclaps, oh-oh chants, story-pop verses) fused with a **Tritonal-style euphoric
festival drop** (soaring supersaws, lush chords, sidechain pump, four-on-the-floor). Fun, nostalgic, hands-up, huge.

- **Tempo:** ~**128 BPM** (four-on-the-floor; bright party verses, euphoric drop).
- **Key:** **A minor** (euphoric-emotional on the drop, still bright and playful on the verses).
- **Vocal:** playful, confident **female lead** (Katy-Perry character) — cheeky and rhythmic on verses, big
  and euphoric on the drop, with **oh-oh gang chants + claps**. Clean, fun, all-ages. (Male or duet swap noted.)

- **Male or duet:** swap `playful female lead` → `playful male lead` or `male-female trade-off`.
