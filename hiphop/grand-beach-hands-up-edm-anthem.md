# Grand Beach (Hands Up) *(minimal-lyric festival EDM anthem — no Latin, no story-pop)*

*A **third, simpler** Grand Beach cut — not the Latin "Hips Don't Lie" version, not the Katy-Perry
story-pop version. This one strips the lyrics way down and leans **fully into the EDM drop**: a huge
festival future-bass build, soaring supersaws, sidechain pump — and a hook that's just the title plus two
images (dancing in the dark / sand like silk), chanted until it's stuck in your head. Same trick "Patio
Nights" and "This Is The One" use — repeat the truth, not the plot. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief:** the other two Grand Beach cuts were "meh" — too many words. Strip it down: minimal lyrics,
> mostly just something catchy and earworm-y, more EDM. Model: the repetition device from **Patio Nights**
> (title chanted as the hook) and **This Is The One** (one truth-line repeated) — but pure festival EDM,
> not pop or hip-hop.

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** cut the story out almost entirely. Two short 4-line verses just set the scene (fire, sand,
dark, dunes) — everything else is the **chorus doing the work**, and the chorus is deliberately tiny: the
title said twice, then two images, then a wordless "whoa-oh" hook. No narrative arc, no bridge full of
lyrics — just a **hook built to loop in your head**, carried by a genuinely huge drop.
**The design rule:** if a line doesn't survive being shouted by a crowd who's never heard the song before,
it gets cut. Production (the drop) does the heavy lifting; the words just need to be repeatable.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **festival future-bass / progressive-house EDM** — huge euphoric build/drop, soaring supersaw
leads, lush sidechained pads, deep sub bass, white-noise risers, gang vocal chant. Minimal-lyric, hands-up,
main-stage energy. No trumpet, no Latin percussion, no saxophone, no story-pop guitar.

- **Tempo:** ~**128 BPM** (full four-on-the-floor festival house — no half-time verse this time).
- **Key:** **A minor** (emotional-euphoric, festival-anthemic).
- **Vocal:** confident, **gender-flexible lead** (female or male both work) — short, punchy phrasing on the
  verse, big unison/gang-chant on the hook, wordless "whoa-oh" ad-libs carrying the drop. Clean, huge, simple.

## ⭐ Best Suno prompt
```
Festival future-bass EDM, 128 BPM, A minor, huge euphoric drop, soaring supersaw leads, sidechain pump, deep sub bass, white-noise risers, gang vocal chant hook, minimal lyrics, hands-up anthem
```
**Tip:** the whole point is restraint — don't let Suno pad the verses. Keep `minimal lyrics` in the prompt
and resist the urge to add more descriptive words to the lyric sheet itself; the shortness IS the feature.
If the drop isn't massive enough → add `bigger festival drop, wobble sub, huge supersaw stack`. If it feels
too bare → add `lush pads, atmospheric breakdown` (production only, not more words). Want a different
two-image hook → swap `dancing in the dark` / `sand like silk` for any other pair from the catalog (driftwood
fire, twelve-story dunes, no signal).
