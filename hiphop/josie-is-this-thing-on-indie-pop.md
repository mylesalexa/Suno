# Josie (Is This Thing On?) *(dreamy indie-pop that flips into bright electro-pop — a love letter with a mic joke)*

*One-line concept: a flustered crush tries to tell someone he likes her and keeps getting it wrong: a text deleted eleven times, a coffee order he can't pronounce, a walk into a door. ## Concept & Angle

**Concept:** The mic is the joke and the metaphor. Tapping a mic and asking "is this thing on?" is the most awkward moment of any speech, and it's exactly how it feels to start telling someone you like them. The intro is the tap. The chorus is the heart as a mic "turned up high." The bridge answers the doubt gently ("Maybe the room's too loud — that's okay") before the song comes roaring back.

**Four things that make it work in this lane:**
1. **The switch is the hook.** Hushed piano and a close vocal, then a drum tick and a retro guitar line, then the bounce. The surprise of the genre flip is what makes the chorus feel like a release.
2. **Specific, funny details instead of generic love-song language:** eleven deleted drafts, rehearsing on the dog, a coffee order he can't spell and is "drinking still."
3. **A built-in sound cue.** "(Tap, tap!)" gives the chorus a hand gesture and an ad-lib that fans can echo; "Hey, hey, hey" is a simple shout-back.
4. **It stays kind and clean.** "I like you a lot / that's all that I've got" is sweet, not intense, and the bridge says there's no rush.

**Name:** "Josie" is a placeholder-style name with a soft two-syllable sound that fits the melody. You can swap it for any two-syllable name (see Variations).

## Style / Tempo / Vocal

**Genre:** **dreamy indie-pop / alt-pop** that flips from an intimate piano opening to bright electro-pop with an **80s analog synth**, a **retro guitar line**, and bouncy drums.

- **Tempo:** **118 BPM** after the switch (my choice, not from the reference). The intro should feel looser and slower than that, almost rubato.
- **Key:** **D major**, bright and easy for a warm male voice.
- **Vocal:** warm, slightly nervous **male lead**: close-mic and tender in the intro and bridge, then smiling and playful on the chorus. Gang vocals only on the final "Hey, hey, hey."
- **Length:** aims for **under three minutes**, like the reference; the lyric is short (2198 characters).
