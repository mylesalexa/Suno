# Porch Light (Right Here the Whole Time) *(dreamy indie-pop that flips from hushed to bright — nameless friends-to-lovers, male lead)*

*One-line concept: the indie version of the small-town friends-to-lovers song, with the name taken out. ## Concept & Angle

**Concept:** One realization, delivered twice: in the pre-chorus ("Wait — hold on — how did I miss it?") and in the chorus ("You were right here, right here the whole time"). The setting (one stoplight, a diner, a creek, a tire swing) makes the idea feel lived in. The porch light is the quiet symbol: it was on the whole time and he never looked.

**Four things that make it work:**
1. **A concrete turning point:** a laugh on a tailgate under fireworks.
2. **Specific small-town details** that read as real.
3. **A bridge that names the real risk:** "Twenty years is a lot to put on the line."
4. **The callback ending:** the waitress's joke comes back from the best friend.
