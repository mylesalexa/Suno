# Kept the Faith

*Christian worship pop-punk — the endurance anthem. The bittersweet-triumphant "last song of the night,"
arms around each other, looking back over the whole journey. Built on Paul's farewell in 2 Timothy 4:7 —
"I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith." It completes the record's
walk through 2 Timothy: the deception of chapter 3 answered by the faithful finish of chapter 4.*

---

## Creative Direction

**Topic:** *Perseverance to the end — "I'll fight the good fight, finish the race, keep the faith — and I
won't do it alone."* It's the song that looks back at every scar and forward to the finish line and the
crown, and insists we cross it together.

**Why it works:** the album's two heaviest message tracks ("Lovers of the Light," "Ever Learning") come
from 2 Timothy 3. Closing the run on 2 Timothy 4:7 lands a quiet, powerful bow — Paul's last words become
the band's. It also resolves the "stand firm / hold on / don't be deceived" thread into "and we made it,"
and answers "Ever Learning's" falling-away with a community that carries each other home.

**Bible verses:**
1. **2 Timothy 4:7–8** — "I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith... there is in store for me the crown of righteousness." *(the entire chorus)*
2. **Hebrews 12:1–2** — "run with perseverance the race marked out for us, fixing our eyes on Jesus." *(pre-chorus + "cloud before me")*
3. **2 Timothy 4:6** — "I am already being poured out like a drink offering." *(verse 2)*
4. **Matthew 25:21** — "Well done, good and faithful servant." *(pre-chorus 2 — what we're running for)*
5. **Revelation 2:10** — "Be faithful, even to the point of death, and I will give you the crown of life." *(the crown)*
6. **Revelation 4:10** — the elders "lay their crowns before the throne." *(the bridge / outro)*
7. **Galatians 6:9** — "Let us not become weary in doing good, for at the proper time we will reap." *(perseverance)*

**Emotional arc:** looking back at the miles and scars, realizing grace carried him (V1) → "I'll run this
race, eyes on the One who never let me drop" (pre) → the anthem: fight, run, keep the faith, won't go it
alone (chorus) → some fell away, but I'll cross the line still holding hope (V2) → "all I'm running for is
'well done'" (pre 2) → the communal bridge: we carry each other home (bridge) → huge final chorus → warm
crowd outro: "we'll finish this together."

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal

**Genre:** driving melodic punk rock — fast, raw, and communal. Distorted power-chord guitars, pounding
punk drums, and a big unison crowd gang-chant "we" chorus. The arms-around-each-other, fists-up closer feel.

- **Tempo:** ~**150–154 BPM** — fast, driving punk-rock pulse; relentless and communal.
- **Key:** **D** major, lift to **E** for the final chorus.
- **Vocal:** warm, passionate male lead — reflective in the verses, full and triumphant in the chorus.
  Big communal gang vocals on the "keep the faith / all the way home" hooks and the bridge — this is a
  whole-band, whole-crowd song.
