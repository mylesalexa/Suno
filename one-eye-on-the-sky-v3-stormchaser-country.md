# One Eye on the Sky — V3 (Act of God)

*Storm-chaser, version 3. The chorus and the bones are the original (you loved them) — this version weaves
**lightning, hail, "act of God," and a foxhole prayer** through the verses, pre-choruses, and the bridge.
Same moody, polished modern-country voice as the catalog. Dark and driving, hits fast.*

> **What changed from the original:** chorus is **unchanged**. V1 gets one lightning touch ("doin' ninety
> toward the lightnin'"); pre-chorus 1 adds a prayer ("sayin' a prayer that God'll cover me"); V2 adds hail
> + lightning ("hail come down like gunfire, lightnin' rippin' through the wind") and a prayer in the run;
> pre-chorus 2 names it an "act of God"; the bridge adds the act-of-God + "Lord, I'm trustin' You" prayer.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal

**Genre:** driving, *moody* modern country — radio/arena country, *not* rock or grunge. Acoustic guitar,
dark brooding electric, pedal steel over a fast, punchy country beat. Big, dramatic chorus with tight
harmonies. Shadowy, not sunny — no banjo.

- **Tempo:** ~**150 BPM** — fast, driving, with weight; **hit the first verse fast.**
- **Key:** **E minor** — dark, the chorus opens big but stays heavy.
- **Vocal:** warm, powerful, *polished* modern-country male lead — clean and strong, big and emotive (the
  same anthem voice as "Everybody Knows Somebody" / "Where We Belong," **not** gritty or grungy). Urgent in
  the verses; the prayer lines should land desperate but full.

Antennas on the dash, three screens glowin' in the dark,
Doin' ninety toward the lightnin' 'fore the first drop even starts.
A caravan of strangers all chasin' the same line,

There's a needle in my blood that only this can feed —

One eye on the road, one eye on the sky,

You floor it, then you run, and you pray that you're fast.
Hundred-mile-an-hour heart, scared out of my mind —
Lord, there's nothin' like the chase when the sky comes alive.
