## 2. Suno Music Prompt (short)

> Anthemic Canadian folk-rock campfire singalong. Acoustic guitar, fiddle, stomps and claps, group gang vocals on the chorus. Warm, rowdy, nostalgic, slightly drunk and joyful. East Coast kitchen-party energy with a big arena-folk chorus. Live-around-the-fire feel, organic and unpolished. Male lead with crowd harmonies.

---

## 3. Recommended Genre / Style

**Canadian folk-rock / Celtic-tinged roots anthem** — kitchen-party singalong meets arena campfire chorus. Think the lineage of Great Big Sea, Spirit of the West, and The Tragically Hip, with Stan Rogers' communal a-cappella warmth in the bridge/outro.

Instrumentation: acoustic guitar (driving strum), fiddle, mandolin or banjo accents, upright/electric bass, light kit drums, hand claps and boot stomps, accordion swell optional. Keep it loose and human.

---

## 4. Tempo & Vocal Feel

- **Tempo:** ~120–128 BPM — brisk, foot-stomping, danceable but easy to sing.
- **Key:** G or D major (open, guitar-friendly, easy for a crowd to belt).
- **Time:** 4/4 with a strong 2-and-4 clap; bridge can drop to half-time before slamming back in.
- **Vocal feel:** Warm, gravelly, slightly weathered male lead — conversational in the verses, wide open and shouted-with-joy in the chorus. Gang/crowd vocals get LOUDER and looser each chorus. The final chorus and outro should sound like everyone at the campsite joined in, harmonies imperfect on purpose.

---

## 5. Alternate Chorus Hook Options

1. **"One more log on the fire, one more round, one more song"** *(primary — title hook)*
2. **"So sing it to the pines, boys, sing it to the stars / These are the best damn nights that ever were ours"**
3. **"Light her up, let her burn 'til the morning / One more round 'round the fire with you"**

5. **"Throw it on the fire and let it roar / Best people I ever knew, give me one night more"**
