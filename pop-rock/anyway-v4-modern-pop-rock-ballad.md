# Anyway *(v4 — emotional modern pop-rock power ballad, "ballad with teeth")*

*A slower, emotional **modern radio pop-rock** love song for my wife — the contrast piece to the three
uptempo tracks. Starts intimate (piano + voice), **builds to a huge belted chorus**. Not the fairytale —
the real one: the flat tires, the hospital chairs, the staying. Romantic and devastating-in-a-good-way,
modern and radio-polished. Female lead (male-lead / duet noted — gorgeous as a him-to-her or trade-off).*

> **Brief:** v4 for contrast — a slow burn with a payoff. Modern radio sound, not 2000s. Lyrics must hit:
> specific, grown, true — the unglamorous proof of love, with a chorus that explodes. Built to make her cry (nicely).

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** love measured not by the big moments but by the **staying** — "I'm a lot to hold, and you held
on anyway." A few years and a few storms in, the proof is the porch light left on. **Why it's not lame:**
**concrete, lived-in images** (the 3 a.m., flat tires, hospital chairs, "I'll know your face" when the
name's gone) instead of greeting-card lines, and a hook that's a vow — *"I'll love you anyway."* Teeth =
the quiet verse detonates into a full-band belt.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **modern radio pop-rock power ballad** — intimate piano + clean guitar in the verses that opens
into a **huge driving chorus** (deep drums, swelling guitars, stacked harmonies); **emotive, polished female
vocal** that goes from a near-whisper to a belt. Cinematic, anthemic, contemporary (not a sappy soft-pop ballad).

- **Tempo:** ~**84 BPM, building** — slow-burn verses, half-time-feel lift, full-throttle final chorus.
- **Key:** **D major** (warm, anthemic lift on the hook).
- **Vocal:** **female lead** — intimate and breathy on verses, powerful and belted on the chorus, big
  stacked harmonies in the final. Modern, emotive, lightly polished. (Male-lead / duet swap noted.)

## ⭐ Best Suno prompt
```
Modern radio pop-rock power ballad, 84 BPM building, key of D, intimate piano into a huge driving chorus, deep drums, emotive polished female vocal, big belted hook, stacked harmonies, cinematic
```
**Tip:** the magic is the **dynamic build** — keep `intimate piano` early and `huge driving chorus` so Suno
contrasts quiet verses with a big payoff. If the chorus stays small → add `explosive chorus, full band,
soaring`. If it gets too soft-pop → add `pop-rock guitars, driving drums`. Too slow to move → nudge to 88.

## Variations
- **Him-to-her (male lead):** swap `female vocal` → `emotive male vocal` so he sings it to her.
- **Duet (most powerful):** `male-female trade verses, harmonize the final chorus` — both voices on "anyway."
- **First-dance / acoustic:** add `acoustic guitar, intimate, sparse` and pull drums back for a wedding cut.
- **Bigger / arena:** add `cinematic strings, gospel-tinged backing choir on the final chorus`.
