# Bay Three, Site Four *(Muse-style alternative rock — cosmic campsite anthem)*

*A real campsite made **cosmic**. Bay Three, Site Four: the tent's up, the boat's tied off, the fire's
going nice, and the sun is setting exactly as the moon is climbing — two lanterns trading shifts across the
sky. Told in the **Muse** lane (soaring falsetto, distorted riff, arpeggiated synths, orchestral lift,
big-dynamics arena build) so an ordinary family night at the lake feels like the center of the universe.
Clean, warm, epic. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief:** make it sound like **Muse** — alternative rock, huge dynamics, Matt-Bellamy falsetto, a
> heavy galloping riff, cosmic imagery — but the subject is dead simple and true: our campsite, the fire,
> the kids, the dog. The trick is scale: the smaller the scene, the bigger we sing it.

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the whole song treats a single family campsite like a **celestial event**. The sun going down
*while* the moon comes up is the cosmic hook Muse is built for — day and night handing off over one small
fire. Everything grounded and specific (tent, boat, beer, kids, the golden doodle) gets sung at arena scale,
so the payoff line lands: *there's nothing in the universe I'd trade this for.*
**The twist:** the second-verse camera pans to the **next site over** — kids scaling the roof of a pickup
like little astronauts touching the moon — then pulls all the way back for the softest, most human line:
**Teddy behind Teddy**, the golden doodle folded in as lumbar support in the fold-out camping chair.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **Muse-style alternative / arena rock** — distorted riff guitar, arpeggiated synth, driving fuzz
bass, thunderous kit, orchestral swells; quiet-restrained verses that **explode** into a soaring falsetto
chorus, plus a heavy galloping (*Knights of Cydonia*) riff section. Space-rock grandeur on a campfire subject.

- **Tempo:** ~**138 BPM** (arena-anthem drive; the riff section can feel double-time/gallop).
- **Key:** **B minor** (dramatic, cinematic, lifts to a bright major on the final chorus).
- **Vocal:** emotive **male lead** — restrained croon on verses, **big soaring falsetto** on the chorus,
  stacked "whoa-oh" gang harmonies on the riff. Clean, epic, never cheesy. (Female-lead swap noted.)

## ⭐ Best Suno prompt
```
Muse-style alternative rock, 138 BPM, B minor, soaring male falsetto, distorted riff guitar, arpeggiated synths, driving fuzz bass, huge dynamics, orchestral swells, galloping riff, singalong chorus
```
**Tip:** lead with `Muse-style` + `soaring male falsetto` + `distorted riff guitar` to lock the Bellamy
character. The magic is **dynamics** — keep verses quiet so the chorus and the `galloping riff` hit huge; if
Suno keeps it flat, add `quiet verse loud chorus, big dynamic contrast`. Want it heavier (*Knights of
Cydonia*) → add `heavy fuzz riff, double-time gallop drums`. Want it more cosmic/orchestral (*Starlight*,
*Uprising*) → add `arpeggiated synth lead, string section, space-rock`. If the falsetto thins out → add
`powerful belted falsetto, stacked harmonies`.

## Variations
- **Heavier (Knights of Cydonia):** push the riff, add `heavy fuzz riff, double-time gallop, guitar solo`, feel ~140+.
- **More cosmic/anthemic (Starlight / Uprising):** add `arpeggiated synth lead, string section, stadium reverb`, let the chorus float.
- **Female lead:** swap `soaring male falsetto` → `soaring female belt` (keep the dynamic contrast).
- **Acoustic campfire cut:** `acoustic alt-rock, fingerpicked guitar, brushed drums, intimate vocal` — same lyrics, unplugged around the actual fire.
- **Title/hook swaps:** "Site Four," "Sun Down, Moon Up," "Canvas Roof," "Best Seats in the Galaxy."
