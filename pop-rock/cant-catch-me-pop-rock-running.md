# Can't Catch Me *(female-led pop-rock / dance-pop running anthem)*

*An uptempo, clap-heavy, fists-up **running anthem** for a wife who runs — built from her current faves
(Avril/Simple Plan pop-punk attitude, Little Mix "Salute" girl-group chant, Meghan Trainor & Olly Murs
retro-pop swing, "Cake by the Ocean"/Bellas bounce). Female lead + gang "hey!" vocals, bright crunchy
guitars, four-on-the-floor, huge singalong chorus. Playful, confident, romantic-but-cheeky — made for running and smiling.*

> **Brief:** more uptempo running songs in her lane. Bright punchy pop-rock / dance-pop, ~128–142 BPM,
> female lead or gang-vocal, claps, pop-punk guitars, big "move your body" chorus. Fun, flirty, empowering,
> romantic **but not sappy** — the kind you can chant at mile three.

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** a confident, cheeky **running anthem** sung from her POV — laces double-knotted, heart pounding,
nobody's catching her. **The hook works on two levels:** literally *you can't catch me* (she's flying), and
*you already caught me once — now keep up.* That double meaning keeps it **romantic without being sappy**:
the love is the fuel and the punchline, not a ballad. Empowering for the run, flirty for the marriage.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** bright **pop-rock / dance-pop running anthem** — crunchy pop-punk rhythm guitars, bouncy
dance-rock bass, crisp handclaps, four-on-the-floor stadium-pop drums; **female lead with gang chant
backing vocals**, huge singalong chorus, "hey!" moments. Early-2000s pop-punk attitude × girl-group chant ×
retro-pop swing × modern radio polish.

- **Tempo:** ~**136 BPM** (the sweet spot; lives comfortably 132–140 for running cadence).
- **Key:** **E major** (bright, lifting, guitar-friendly).
- **Vocal:** confident, playful **female lead** — punchy and rhythmic on verses, big and anthemic on the
  chorus, with **group "hey!" / na-na gang vocals**. Light polish fine. Romantic but cheeky, never sappy.
  (Duet swap noted — `male-female call and response` for a couples version.)

**Tip:** lead with `pop-rock` + `pop-punk guitars` so it stays guitar-forward (not pure EDM), and keep
`female lead` up front with `male lead` in Exclude to lock the vocal. The `gang chant vocals` + `handclaps`
are what give it the Little Mix / Bellas singalong energy. Too soft → add `bigger drums, stadium pop`.
Want more retro swing (Olly Murs / Meghan Trainor) → add `retro pop swing, punchy brass stabs`.

## Variations
- **Couples / duet edit:** swap `female lead` → `male-female call and response` for a run-together version.
- **More dance, less rock:** drop the guitars down, add `dance-pop synths, four-on-the-floor, club energy`.
- **More retro-soul (Trainor/Murs):** add `retro pop swing, doo-wop backing vocals, brass stabs`, pull to ~132.
- **More pop-punk (Avril/Simple Plan):** push guitars up, add `palm-muted power chords, punchy punk drums`, ~140.
- **Title/hook swaps if you want a different chant:** "Keep Up," "Lace 'Em Up," "Catch Me If You Can," "Heart Rate."
