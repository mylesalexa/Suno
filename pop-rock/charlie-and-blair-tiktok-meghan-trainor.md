## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** turn the friendship anthem into a **TikTok challenge**. Meghan Trainor's superpower is the
retro-pop *swing* + finger-snap groove + a hook so simple you're singing it after one listen ("Made You
Look," "All About That Bass"). So the whole song is engineered around a **spell-it-out chant — "B-F-F, that's
you and me"** — plus an explicit dance callout (*snap-snap, clap-clap, left, left, right, hands in the air*)
that becomes the TikTok choreo. Names still in the hook, so it's unmistakably *their* video.
**The payoff:** the bridge drops the origin story (babies in a double stroller → both landing the big part in
*Artista*) then slams back into the dance for a final, key-lifted chorus.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **Meghan-Trainor-style TikTok dance-pop** — retro doo-wop swing, finger snaps, handclaps, brass
stabs, bouncy upright/electric bass, doo-wop "ooh" backing vocals; sassy agile female lead, chant hook, huge
danceable clap-along chorus and a copy-me post-chorus. Cheeky, confident, radio-clean, made to move.

- **Tempo:** ~**122 BPM** (snappy dance groove with a retro shuffle; lives 118–126).
- **Key:** **C major** (bright, brassy, sing-along; lifts a step on the final chorus).
- **Vocal:** sassy, **agile female lead** — playful and rhythmic on verses, big and confident on the chorus,
  with **group snaps/claps + doo-wop "ooh" backing** and gang chant shouts. Clean, cheeky, fun.
  (Duet-lead swap noted so Charlie and Blair trade lines.)
