*A bratty, vivid early-2000s **pop-punk love anthem** — Avril Lavigne snarl on the lead, **Simple Plan
featured** on verse 2 and a full duet bridge. Not about running: it's the beautiful-disaster love song —
"on paper we make no sense, in real life we're unstoppable." Cheeky, defiant, romantic-but-never-sappy,
built to be screamed in the car. Female lead + featured male, distorted power chords, gang-vocal chorus.*

> **Brief:** more Avril, with Simple Plan in it. Lyrics must NOT be lame — specific, cinematic, with

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the love song for the couple everyone *warned* — two beautiful disasters who shouldn't work and
absolutely do. Years in, still each other's best worst decision. **Why it's not lame:** it's built on
**specific, cinematic images** (heart spilled on her favorite jeans, 2 a.m. with a half-charged phone, a
mess in a band tee) and a **twist** — "everybody said don't, so we did." Romantic via *defiance*, not
mush. The **Simple Plan feature** answers from his side (the never-grow-up underdog), so it's a real duet.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **early-2000s pop-punk / pop-rock** — distorted palm-muted power chords, punchy punk drums,
bouncy bass, handclaps, big gang-vocal chorus; **bratty female lead** (Avril snarl) with a **featured male
vocal** (Simple Plan earnest-underdog) trading on V2 + the bridge. Anthemic, melodic, a little snotty-sweet.

- **Tempo:** ~**152 BPM** (Sk8er-Boi / I'm-Just-a-Kid pocket — fast, driving, fist-pump).
- **Key:** **E major** (bright, guitar-forward).
- **Vocal:** confident **female lead**, conversational and bratty on verses, soaring on the chorus;
  **featured male** lead on V2 and call-and-response on the bridge; stacked gang vocals + "oh-oh-oh" hooks.
