# Sweet Tooth *(v5 — retro-swing dance-pop banger, runnable energy, NOT about running)*

*The re-centered running-energy track: uptempo, punchy, claps-and-brass **retro-swing dance-pop** in the
lane we hadn't tapped yet — Meghan Trainor / Olly Murs swing + DNCE "Cake by the Ocean" bounce + Little Mix
gang-chant. Perfect run pace (~134 BPM), but the **lyrics aren't about running at all** — they're a flirty,
sassy, grown love-bop for my wife: that addicted, can't-get-enough, giddy feeling. Female lead, fun and cheeky.*

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** a cheeky "sweet tooth" anthem — she's unapologetically addicted to her person and not even
trying to quit. **Why it's not lame:** a **specific, playful craving metaphor** carried all the way through
(top-shelf candy, the dentist's warning, 3 a.m. cravings, "cavities and confetti") instead of generic gushing —
flirty and grown, with a chorus made to shout. Romantic by way of *appetite*, not mush.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **retro-swing dance-pop** — punchy brass stabs, doo-wop handclaps and finger snaps, bouncy
funk bass, bright guitars; **sassy female lead** with **gang chant backing vocals**, huge singalong chorus.
Modern polish over a classic soul-pop swing — fun, flirty, danceable, run-ready.

- **Tempo:** ~**134 BPM** — bouncy swing-pop in the running pocket.
- **Key:** **C major** (bright, classic, sunny).
- **Vocal:** confident, playful **female lead** — sassy and rhythmic on verses, big and joyful on the chorus,
  with group "hey!" gang vocals and horn-section call-and-response. (Duet swap noted.)
