# Before the Sun *(Track 1 — pre-dawn drive up the coast, hushed lo-fi)*

*Album opener. The literal dawn patrol — up before sunrise, coffee in the console, headlights cutting
through the marine layer on an empty stretch of coast highway. Hushed, bedroom-4-track lo-fi, almost no
percussion. Ends with the sun actually rising. Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the quiet before everything else on the album happens — dark highway, thermos, radio hum,
that specific SoCal-morning gray (locals call it the marine layer, or "June gloom" when it lingers) before
it burns off. **The gimmick:** the song ends with the sunrise actually happening — one warm chord bloom as
the tape hiss thins — handing straight into Track 2.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **hushed lo-fi surf ballad** — warm pad, sparse acoustic guitar, minimal brushed percussion,
bedroom-4-track tape hiss, a barely-there car-radio hum. Quiet, patient, almost ambient.

- **Tempo:** ~**66 BPM** (very slow, patient).
- **Key:** **D major** (warm, hushed).
- **Vocal:** hushed, half-awake **lead vocal** (male or female) — close-mic'd, breathy, barely more than a murmur until the end.
