# Duck Dive *(Track 2 — paddling out through the impact zone, rhythmic lo-fi)*

*Not the peaceful part — the work part. Getting from the shore to the lineup means pushing through the
impact zone, where the waves are actually breaking, and that means duck diving: shove the nose under, knee
on the tail, hold your breath, let the wave's whole force pass overhead. Researched for accuracy — this is
the real technique, not just a mood. Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the physical, rhythmic grind between "arriving" (Before the Sun) and "floating, arrived"
(Salt Air) — a song about the actual technique of getting out there. Duck diving works by diving as far
beneath the wave's energy as possible: start the dive **5 to 7 feet before the wave hits**, push the
board's nose under with the rails, then drive a knee (or back foot, on a longer board) down on the tail so
the whole board submerges evenly — not just the nose — forming one continuous arc from air to water and
back to air. **The gimmick:** the song itself has a tense, pulsing rhythm every time a wave passes overhead,
then releases into a warmer, calmer key once the impact zone is behind you.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **lo-fi rhythmic surf-rock** — pulsing tremolo guitar, tape hiss, a rhythmic paddling-pulse
percussion pattern, tension that resolves into warmth. Physical and a little odd — the song has a real
heartbeat.

- **Tempo:** ~**82 BPM**, tense and pulsing in the verses, settling once the lineup is reached.
- **Key:** **A minor** in the verses (tension), resolving to **A major** in the outro (past the break).
- **Vocal:** breathless, **physical lead vocal** (male or female) — clipped phrasing, like talking between strokes.
