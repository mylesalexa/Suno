# The Lineup *(Track 3 — waiting your turn, dry lo-fi)*

*You made it past the break — now you wait. The lineup runs on an unwritten pecking order: whoever's
closest to the peak gets the wave, nobody drops in on somebody already riding, and if you blow it, you go
to the back of the line. Researched for accuracy — this is real etiquette, not a made-up rule. A new guy
paddles straight for the peak anyway. Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the patience and quiet social order of sitting in the lineup — the surfer closest to the peak
has priority, you don't paddle around someone to steal it ("snaking"), and if you're new you start on the
shoulder and work your way in. **The comedy:** a "kook" (a clueless newbie) ignores all of it and drops in
on a local's wave anyway — everybody just shakes their head, nobody makes a scene.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **dry lo-fi surf-bum pop** — gently bobbing rhythm (mirrors sitting on a board), warm guitar
under cassette tape hiss, brushed drums. Patient, wry, deadpan — not comedic in a broad way, just dry.

- **Tempo:** ~**76 BPM** (a gentle, rocking bob — like sitting on a board between sets).
- **Key:** **G major** (warm, easygoing).
- **Vocal:** dry, observational **lead vocal** (male or female) — patient, a little amused, never annoyed out loud.
