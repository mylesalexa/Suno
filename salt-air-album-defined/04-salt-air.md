# Salt Air *(Track 4 — morning paddle-out, lo-fi surf groove)*

*Wax on the board, salt in the hair, no watch, no plans — just the wave, the coast highway behind you, and
the marine layer starting to burn off. Stripped-back, sunlit, intentionally unpolished — the song itself
slows down in the middle, like floating, before paddling back in. Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** a song about nothing, on purpose — waxing the board, paddling out, losing track of time,
floating. **The structural trick:** the song slows down mid-track — a stripped-back, half-time "floating"
breakdown (just guitar, voice, and tape hiss) — before drums and bass paddle back in for the final chorus.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **retro lo-fi surf-rock** — reverb-drenched tremolo guitar, cassette tape hiss under everything,
loose brushed drums, handclaps. Unpolished on purpose — room to breathe, not radio-glossy.

- **Tempo:** ~**104 BPM** main groove, **dropping to a loose half-time float** in the breakdown.
- **Key:** **A major** (bright, warm, guitar-friendly).
- **Vocal:** laid-back, breezy, **unpolished lead** (male or female) — almost talk-sung on the verses.
