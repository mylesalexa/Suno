# Small Craft Advisory *(Track 5 — grainy weather report opener, slow lo-fi)*

*Opens on a genuinely low-quality AM marine-conditions broadcast — the real, dry, slightly odd cadence of
a coastal weather report — then settles into a slow, deadpan song about trusting that same crackly
forecast more than your own eyes. Not upbeat, not a "hype" song — dry, warm, a little strange, the way an
actual early-morning ritual is. Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the daily ritual of checking the surf report, played completely deadpan instead of as a hype
anthem — repeating the report back almost word for word, half-asleep, half-devoted. **The gimmick:** the
genuine low-quality marine-forecast broadcast opens the track and drifts back in once more, so the whole
thing plays like something you'd actually catch tuning across an early AM dial.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **slow lo-fi surf-bum pop** — warm jangly guitar buried under cassette tape hiss, garbled
AM-radio texture, brushed drums. Dry and a little odd rather than upbeat — deadpan devotion, not hype.

- **Tempo:** ~**84 BPM** (slow, unhurried, almost sleepy).
- **Key:** **E major** (bright underneath the grain, but the tempo keeps it laid-back).
- **Vocal:** dry, deadpan, **half-awake lead vocal** (male or female) — reciting the report almost like scripture.
