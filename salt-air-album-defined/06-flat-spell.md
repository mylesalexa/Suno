# Flat Spell *(Track 6 — voicemail intro, lazy midday lull)*

*The report promised swell, but the ocean's dead calm — a whole midday spent waiting it out: cards at the
shop, the cam checked for the ninth time, a dog with the right idea. Opens on a voicemail. Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the "hurry up and wait" side of surf culture. **The gimmick:** a genuine voicemail opens the
track, and mid-song the whole thing goes almost silent — an intentional "waiting" hold — before a phone
buzz cuts through.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **lazy lo-fi surf shuffle** — warm shuffle groove, light brushed drums, tape hiss, tinny
compressed phone-call texture on the intro. Bored but content — a held breath, not a low point.

- **Tempo:** ~**78 BPM** (slow, lazy shuffle).
- **Key:** **C major** (warm, easygoing).
- **Vocal:** dry, wry, **conversational lead vocal** (male or female) — mildly bored, amused by its own boredom.
