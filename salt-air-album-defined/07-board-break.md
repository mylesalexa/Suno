# Board Break *(Track 7 — vintage instrumental surf-rock, now properly lo-fi)*

*The one deliberately vintage-energetic moment on the record — true to real 60s instrumental surf-rock
heritage (fast, twangy, almost wordless), but dressed in the same cassette grain as everything else on
this pressing so it never breaks the album's lo-fi identity. Wipes out mid-track, recovers. Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the purest "meaningless surfer music" on the album — no lyrics to speak of, a fast twangy
guitar theme that IS the wave, now run through tape hiss and warble so it sounds like a scratched vinyl
side, not a clean modern recording. **The gimmick:** midway through, the song wipes out — the beat
tumbles, the guitar crashes into reverb-drenched dissonance — then paddles back into the final theme.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **lo-fi vintage instrumental surf-rock** — fast tremolo-picked reverb guitar buried under
cassette tape hiss and vinyl crackle, driving tom-heavy surf beat, walking bass, minimal-to-no lead vocal.

- **Tempo:** ~**152 BPM** (brisk, driving — classic instrumental surf pace, kept deliberately fast as the
  album's one authentic vintage-energy outlier).
- **Key:** **E major** (bright, twangy, underneath the grain).
- **Vocal:** almost entirely **instrumental** — shouted ad-libs, filtered and buried in the mix.
