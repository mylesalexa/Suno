# Forty Summers *(Track 7 — grainy local-newscast interview opener, warm lo-fi)*

*Opens on a garbled local-news human-interest segment — an anchor's brief lead-in, then a clip of an old
local surfer being interviewed about how long he's been coming to this beach. Then a warm tribute song to
the guy who's outlasted every trend at the coast: same truck, same spot, forty summers running.
Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** every beach town has one — the guy who's been paddling out longer than most locals have been
alive, unbothered by condos or crowds. **The gimmick:** a genuine low-quality local-newscast clip opens the
track — anchor voice-over, then the interview — so it plays like something actually aired on a slow news
day.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **warm lo-fi acoustic surf-pop** — acoustic guitar with light tremolo electric accents, warm
cassette tape hiss, brushed drums. Nostalgic but not sad — proud, a little wry.

- **Tempo:** ~**92 BPM** (relaxed, warm).
- **Key:** **G major** (warm, nostalgic).
- **Vocal:** warm, storytelling **lead vocal** (male or female) — third-person narrator, fond and a little amused.
