# Ding Repair *(Track 8 — the resin ritual, hazy lo-fi)*

*Board Break ended in a wipeout — this is the aftermath. Clean the wax and sand off, check for water
trapped inside, mix the resin, press the bubbles out, cover it in plastic to hold the shape, and wait.
Researched for accuracy: real repairs need real cure time — small dings need at least an hour, full cure
runs 6–8 hours or more, and it has to sit out of direct sun. Hazy, patient, a little odd. Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the unglamorous, meditative ritual after the exciting part is over — a garage, a bottle of
resin, the smell, and nothing to do but wait. **Why it earns its place:** every surf album has the wave; not
many have the part where you fix what the wave broke. Patience as its own kind of surf-culture nuance.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **hazy lo-fi surf-bum pop** — warm, slightly warped guitar (mirroring resin-fume haziness),
cassette tape hiss, garage ambience, minimal percussion. Slow, patient, a little strange.

- **Tempo:** ~**74 BPM** (slow, patient, unhurried).
- **Key:** **Bb major** (warm, slightly hazy).
- **Vocal:** relaxed, half-distracted **lead vocal** (male or female) — talking through a chore, not performing.
