# Open Til the Swell Comes In *(Track 9 — life inside the surf shop, lo-fi)*

*A day in the life of a surf shop: wax on the counter, boards on the rack, the regular who never buys
anything, the tourist who can't pick a board, a dog asleep in the sun, a radio crackling the surf report.
The song ends mid-word as the door slams. Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the surf shop as its own little world. **The payoff line — and the shop's whole philosophy —
is the title:** the sign says "open," but the real hours are dictated by the surf report, not the clock.
**The gimmick:** the song doesn't fade out — the swell report crackles in, everyone bolts, and the track
cuts off mid-word as the door slams.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **lo-fi surf-bum pop** — warm jangly guitar under cassette tape hiss, door chime bell, crackling
AM-radio texture, brushed drums. Breezy, character-driven, unhurried.

- **Tempo:** ~**100 BPM** (relaxed shop-day groove).
- **Key:** **G major** (bright, warm, easygoing).
- **Vocal:** breezy, conversational **lead vocal** (male or female) — almost telling a story to a friend.
