# The Glass *(Track 10 — the wind science behind dawn patrol, quiet lo-fi)*

*Not just a feeling — this is the actual reason "dawn patrol" exists. At night, land cools faster than the
ocean, so cool air sinks and flows out toward the warmer water — a light offshore wind that smooths the
wave face into glass. As the sun heats the land, that pattern flips to an onshore sea breeze, usually
somewhere between late morning and early afternoon, and the glass turns to chop. Researched for accuracy.
A quiet piece of old-timer wisdom before Forty Summers. Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the technical, almost meditative knowledge that underlies the whole album's premise —
explained plainly, like an old-timer passing it down rather than a lecture. **Placement:** right before
Forty Summers, so the album's "wisdom passed down" thread runs two tracks in a row — the knowledge, then
the man who embodies it.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **quiet lo-fi surf-folk** — warm acoustic-leaning guitar under cassette tape hiss, minimal
percussion, wind ambience. Contemplative, plainspoken, unhurried.

- **Tempo:** ~**70 BPM** (slow, thoughtful).
- **Key:** **D major** (warm, clear).
- **Vocal:** quiet, plainspoken **lead vocal** (male or female) — explaining something simple and true.
