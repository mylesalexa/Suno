# Home Movie *(Track 12 — grainy old-tape recap, warped lo-fi)*

*The song sounds like it's actually playing off a warped cassette of a childhood beach day. Grainy, warm,
a little broken. The tape warps and "rewinds" mid-song before snapping back in. Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the song *is* the old tape — cassette hiss, vinyl crackle, warped wow-and-flutter pitch,
muffled AM-radio mono quality, like the audio is bleeding through a Super-8 home movie of a July day along
the coast. **The gimmick:** the pitch bends down like a dying tape motor, a rewind whir plays, then it
snaps back in brighter.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **lo-fi retro surf pop** — cassette tape hiss, vinyl crackle, warped wow-and-flutter pitch,
muffled AM-radio mono quality, reverb tremolo surf guitar, loose brushed drums.

- **Tempo:** ~**98 BPM**, with a **tape-warp/rewind effect** mid-song.
- **Key:** **D major** (warm, bright, guitar-friendly).
- **Vocal:** breezy, **unpolished lead** (male or female) — slightly buried in the mix, warm and close-mic'd.
