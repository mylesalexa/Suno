# Tiki Torch Nights *(Track 13 — evening lounge wind-down, now properly lo-fi)*

*The evening after — torches lit down by the pier, feet in the sand, a slow marimba melody drifting over
the boardwalk bar. Vibraphone and marimba take over from guitar as the lead voice; congas and bongos
replace the surf-beat drums — a different instrumental world, now dressed in the same tape grain as the
rest of the record. Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** if Salt Air is the morning paddle-out, this is the evening after — still no plot, still no
lesson, just warm imagery of a beach evening winding down along the coast. **The fix from the original
cut:** the main version of this song was missing tape texture entirely — added here so it holds the
album's lo-fi identity as strictly as every other track.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **lo-fi retro tiki-lounge exotica** — vibraphone and marimba lead melody under cassette tape
hiss and vinyl crackle, congas and bongos, upright bass, light tremolo guitar accents. Slow, warm, unhurried.

- **Tempo:** ~**86 BPM** (slow, swaying, lounge pace).
- **Key:** **F major** (warm, mellow, lounge-friendly).
- **Vocal:** relaxed, breezy **lead vocal** (male or female) — soft, close-mic'd, almost a lullaby delivery.
