# Night Swim *(Track 14 — the close, hushed and now properly lo-fi)*

*The final track. Heavy shimmering reverb, brushed slow drums, warm pads, a hushed close vocal — now with
cassette tape hiss woven through it, the last texture fix needed to hold the album's lo-fi identity strict
all the way to the end. No big chorus payoff, no loud final section — it stays quiet the whole way through,
on purpose. Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** floating on your back in dark water at night — the lifeguard tower a black silhouette against
the moon, a distant dying bonfire on the shore, everyone quiet. **The structural choice:** most songs build
to something bigger; this one deliberately doesn't.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **hushed dreamy lo-fi surf ballad** — heavy shimmering reverb, cassette tape hiss, brushed slow
drums, warm analog pads, light tremolo guitar. Restrained the entire way through.

- **Tempo:** ~**68 BPM** (slow, floating, unhurried).
- **Key:** **E minor** (moody but warm — nocturnal, not sad).
- **Vocal:** hushed, intimate **lead vocal** (male or female) — close, breathy, almost whispered.
