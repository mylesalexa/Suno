# Board Break (Lo-Fi Tape Version) *(the mashup — Board Break through Home Movie's grainy tape production)*

*Same fast, almost-wordless instrumental surf-rock theme, same **wipeout-and-recovery** structure, now
run through the folder's degraded old-tape treatment: cassette hiss, vinyl crackle, warped wow-and-flutter
pitch, muffled AM-radio mono quality. Bonus: the wipeout section now doubles as actual **tape damage** —
the reel audibly chews and glitches right when the song wipes out. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief:** part of the "make the whole album lo-fi tape" pass — Board Break, dressed in Home Movie's tape sound.

---

## This is a mashup — same theme, new production
The structure and (near-wordless) content are unchanged from `board-break-instrumental-surf.md` — same
Theme A/B, same **wipeout**, same recovery. What changes is entirely the **sound**, borrowed from
`home-movie-lofi-tape-surf.md`: cassette hiss, warped wow-and-flutter pitch, vinyl crackle, muffled AM-radio
mono quality. The wipeout gets an extra layer for free here — a real "tape wipeout" (the reel chewing,
pitch dive-bombing) lands right on top of the musical one.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **lo-fi vintage instrumental surf-rock** — fast tremolo-picked reverb guitar buried under cassette
tape hiss, vinyl crackle, warped wow-and-flutter pitch, muffled AM-radio mono quality, driving tom-heavy
surf beat, walking bass. Bright but grainy — 60s beach movie played off a worn-out cassette.

- **Tempo:** ~**152 BPM** (brisk, driving), with the pitch **wobbling** throughout instead of staying locked.
- **Key:** **E major** (bright, twangy, underneath the grain).
- **Vocal:** almost entirely **instrumental** — shouted ad-libs, now filtered and buried in the mix like
  they're bleeding through an old speaker.
