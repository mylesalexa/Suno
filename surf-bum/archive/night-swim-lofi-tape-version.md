# Night Swim (Lo-Fi Tape Version) *(the mashup — the hushed nocturnal ballad through old-tape production)*

*Same restrained, deliberately-quiet nighttime ballad, now run through the folder's degraded old-tape
treatment. Of everything in this batch, this might be the best fit — a warped, hissy old cassette is
already how a real memory sounds in your head. The tape's imperfection makes the hush feel even more
private. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief:** part of the "make the whole album lo-fi tape" pass — Night Swim, dressed in Home Movie's
> tape sound.

---

## This is a mashup — same words, new production
The lyrics and structure are unchanged from `night-swim-dreamy-surf.md` — same hushed verses, same refusal
to build to a big chorus, same single warm swell near the end. What changes is the **sound**, borrowed from
`home-movie-lofi-tape-surf.md`: cassette hiss, vinyl crackle, warped wow-and-flutter pitch, muffled AM-radio
mono quality. Night Swim's own rule was "restraint IS the production" — the tape hiss doesn't fight that,
it deepens it. A degraded recording of something quiet feels even more like a secret.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **lo-fi hushed dreamy surf ballad** — heavy shimmering reverb buried under cassette tape hiss,
vinyl crackle, warped wow-and-flutter pitch, muffled AM-radio mono quality, brushed slow drums, warm analog
pads. Restrained throughout — no drop, no big final chorus, just grain and hush.

- **Tempo:** ~**68 BPM** (slow, floating), with the pitch **drifting** very gently — never distracting,
  just present.
- **Key:** **E minor** (moody but warm, underneath the grain).
- **Vocal:** hushed, intimate **lead vocal** (male or female) — close, breathy, buried slightly in the mix
  like an old recording of a real memory. Soft wordless "ooh" harmonies.
