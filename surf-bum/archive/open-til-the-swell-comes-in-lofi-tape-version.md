# Open Til the Swell Comes In (Lo-Fi Tape Version) *(the mashup — surf-shop life through old-tape production)*

*Same day-in-the-life-of-the-shop song, same mid-word cutoff ending, now run through the folder's degraded
old-tape treatment. This one has a built-in excuse for it — the shop already had a radio playing in the
original. Here, the whole recording sounds like it WAS the shop radio, taped off a boombox behind the
counter. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief:** part of the "make the whole album lo-fi tape" pass — the surf shop song, dressed in Home
> Movie's tape sound.

---

## This is a mashup — same words, new production
The lyrics and structure are unchanged from `open-til-the-swell-comes-in-surf-shop.md` — same regular, same
tourist, same shop dog, same abrupt door-slam cutoff. What changes is the **sound**, borrowed from
`home-movie-lofi-tape-surf.md`: cassette hiss, vinyl crackle, warped wow-and-flutter pitch, muffled AM-radio
mono quality. It fits the concept twice over — the original already had a crackling radio surf report
inside the song; now the *entire track* sounds like it was recorded off that same radio, on a cassette
somebody left running behind the register.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **lo-fi retro surf-bum pop** — warm jangly guitar buried under cassette tape hiss, vinyl crackle,
warped wow-and-flutter pitch, muffled AM-radio mono quality, door chime bell, brushed drums. Sounds like a
boombox cassette taped behind the counter on a slow afternoon.

- **Tempo:** ~**100 BPM** (relaxed shop-day groove), with the pitch **drifting** gently throughout.
- **Key:** **G major** (bright, warm, underneath the grain).
- **Vocal:** breezy, conversational **lead vocal** (male or female) — buried slightly in the mix, warm and
  a little muffled, like it's coming from a speaker across the shop.
