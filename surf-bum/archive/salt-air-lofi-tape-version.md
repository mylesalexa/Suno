# Salt Air (Lo-Fi Tape Version) *(the mashup — Salt Air's lyrics through Home Movie's grainy tape production)*

*Exactly what it sounds like — **Salt Air's** words and structure, run through **Home Movie's** degraded
old-tape treatment: cassette hiss, vinyl crackle, warped wow-and-flutter pitch, muffled AM-radio mono
quality. Same morning paddle-out, same mid-song float, but it sounds like it's playing off a warped
cassette somebody left in a beach-bag glovebox for ten summers. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief:** a Salt Air version with the lo-fi tape sound from Home Movie. New file — the other two stay
> as they are.

---

## This is a mashup — same words, new production
The lyrics and structure are unchanged from `salt-air-retro-surf.md` — same verses, same "salt air, salt
air" hook, same mid-song **tempo-drop float**. What changes is entirely the **sound**, borrowed from
`home-movie-lofi-tape-surf.md`: cassette hiss under everything, warped wow-and-flutter pitch, vinyl crackle,
muffled AM-radio mono quality. The two structural gimmicks actually complement each other here — Salt Air's
half-time "floating" breakdown, now dressed in tape warble, feels even more like drifting: slow, warm, and
a little unstable, like the tape itself is floating too.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **lo-fi retro surf-bum pop** — reverb-drenched tremolo surf guitar buried under cassette tape
hiss, vinyl crackle, warped wow-and-flutter pitch, muffled AM-radio mono quality, loose brushed drums,
handclaps. Intentionally degraded — the "bad recording" IS the production, same as Home Movie.

- **Tempo:** ~**104 BPM** main groove, **dropping to a loose, warbly half-time float** in the breakdown
  before the tape (and the drums and bass) paddle back in for the final chorus.
- **Key:** **A major** (bright and warm underneath the grain).
- **Vocal:** laid-back, breezy, **unpolished lead** (male or female) — slightly buried in the mix, warm and
  close-mic'd like it's bleeding through an old speaker. Clean, casual, unbothered. (Duet/harmony swap noted.)
