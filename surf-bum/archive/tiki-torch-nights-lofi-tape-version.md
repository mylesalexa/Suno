# Tiki Torch Nights (Lo-Fi Tape Version) *(the mashup — tiki lounge exotica through old-tape production)*

*Same slow evening lounge song, same vibraphone-and-marimba world, now run through the folder's degraded
old-tape treatment. It fits almost *too* well — exotica records from this era genuinely lived on worn
vinyl and cassette, so hearing this one crackle and warp feels less like an effect and more like the
truth. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief:** part of the "make the whole album lo-fi tape" pass — Tiki Torch Nights, dressed in Home
> Movie's tape sound.

---

## This is a mashup — same words, new production
The lyrics and structure are unchanged from `tiki-torch-nights-exotica-surf.md` — same verses, same
vibraphone break, same warm evening wind-down. What changes is the **sound**, borrowed from
`home-movie-lofi-tape-surf.md`: cassette hiss, vinyl crackle, warped wow-and-flutter pitch, muffled
AM-radio mono quality. The vibraphone and marimba — already a slightly detuned, wooden, "old" sound —
carry the warp beautifully; it reads as a vintage exotica record, not a broken modern one.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **lo-fi retro tiki-lounge exotica** — vibraphone and marimba lead buried under cassette tape
hiss, vinyl crackle, warped wow-and-flutter pitch, muffled AM-radio mono quality, congas and bongos, upright
bass. Slow, warm, and worn — a lounge record somebody's been playing for forty summers.

- **Tempo:** ~**86 BPM** (slow, swaying), with the pitch **drifting** gently instead of staying locked.
- **Key:** **F major** (warm, mellow, underneath the grain).
- **Vocal:** relaxed, breezy **lead vocal** (male or female) — soft, slightly buried in the mix, like it's
  bleeding through an old lounge speaker. Light scat "la-la" vocals in the instrumental sections.
