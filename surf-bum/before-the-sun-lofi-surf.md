# Before the Sun *(album opener — quiet pre-dawn drive, hushed lo-fi)*

*Track 1 of **Salt & Static**. The literal "dawn patrol" — up before sunrise, coffee in the console,
headlights cutting through fog on an empty road to the beach. Hushed, bedroom-4-track lo-fi, almost no
percussion. Ends with the sun actually rising — the album's first sunrise, handing straight off into
"Salt Air." Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the quiet before everything else in the album happens. No plot beyond the drive itself — dark
road, thermos, radio hum, anticipation. **The gimmick:** the song ends with the sunrise actually happening —
one warm chord bloom as the tape hiss thins out — so it functions as a literal sunrise cue into the next
track.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **hushed lo-fi surf-adjacent ballad** — warm pad, sparse acoustic guitar, minimal brushed
percussion, bedroom-4-track tape hiss, a barely-there car-radio hum bleeding through. Quiet, patient,
almost ambient.

- **Tempo:** ~**66 BPM** (very slow, patient).
- **Key:** **D major** (warm, but hushed enough to feel pre-dawn).
- **Vocal:** hushed, half-awake **lead vocal** (male or female) — close-mic'd, breathy, barely more than a
  murmur until the very end.
