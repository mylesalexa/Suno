# Board Break *(vintage instrumental surf-rock — fast, twangy, mostly wordless)*

*A third surf-bum flavor — this one's almost entirely **instrumental**, classic vintage 60s surf-rock:
fast reverb-drenched tremolo guitar lead, driving tom-heavy surf beat, walking bass, just a handful of
shouted ad-libs instead of real lyrics. Structural gimmick: the song actually **wipes out** mid-track —
tumbling drums, a crashing dissonant guitar splash — before paddling back into the final theme. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief:** part of the "mix up the vibes" batch — this one leans into pure vintage instrumental surf-rock,
> fast and twangy, almost no lyrics.

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the purest version of "meaningless surfer music" — no lyrics to speak of, just a fast, twangy
guitar theme that IS the wave. **The gimmick:** midway through, the song **wipes out** — the beat tumbles,
the guitar crashes into reverb-drenched dissonance, cymbals splash like water — then it picks itself back
up and paddles into the final theme, a little more triumphant than before.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **vintage instrumental surf-rock** — fast tremolo-picked reverb guitar lead, driving tom-heavy
"surf beat" drums, walking bass, minimal-to-no lead vocal. Bright, brisk, classic 60s beach-movie energy.

- **Tempo:** ~**152 BPM** (brisk, driving — classic instrumental surf pace).
- **Key:** **E major** (bright, twangy, guitar-friendly — the classic surf-rock key).
- **Vocal:** almost entirely **instrumental** — just shouted ad-libs ("Hey!," "Whoa!," a whistle) instead of
  a sung lyric. The guitar lead is the lead vocal. (A version with a few sung lines noted in Variations.)
