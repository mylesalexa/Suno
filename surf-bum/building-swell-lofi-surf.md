# Building Swell *(campfire legend — the album's one big, driving track)*

*Track 12 of **Salt & Static**. Not something happening that day — a story told that night, around the
fire, about the biggest swell anyone in town ever saw. The one deliberately bigger, more driving track on
an otherwise mellow record — the album's single moment of real adrenaline, framed as a legend being
retold rather than a live event. Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** every beach town has one story like this — the day it got too big, the one person who
paddled out anyway, the story that gets bigger with each telling. **Why it earns its place:** the album is
mostly chill on purpose, so one real jolt of energy — clearly marked as a *story*, not the day's actual
events — keeps the record from being wall-to-wall the same tempo.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **driving lo-fi surf-rock** — tremolo guitar building into something bigger than the rest of the
album, driving drums, gang vocals on the chorus, cassette tape hiss under a campfire crackle. The album's
biggest, most energetic track.

- **Tempo:** ~**118 BPM** (the fastest, most driving song on the record besides Board Break).
- **Key:** **E minor** (dramatic — the one minor-key track on the album).
- **Vocal:** a storyteller's **lead vocal** (male or female), building from a hushed campfire-story
  delivery into a big, gang-vocal chorus.
