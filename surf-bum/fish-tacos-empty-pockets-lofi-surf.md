# Fish Tacos & Empty Pockets *(post-session ritual, warm golden-hour lo-fi)*

*Track 9 of **Salt & Static**. The ritual after a good session — starving, broke, piling five deep into a
truck to split a bag of tacos with loose change from the cupholder. Warm, communal, golden-hour light.
Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the meal that tastes better than it should because of who you're eating it with, not what it
costs. No plot beyond that — just the warmth of a shared, broke, happy afternoon.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **warm lo-fi surf-pop with a light taco-truck shuffle** — acoustic-leaning guitar, a warm horn
accent, easy shuffle groove, cassette tape hiss. Golden-hour, communal, unhurried.

- **Tempo:** ~**100 BPM** (easy shuffle).
- **Key:** **D major** (warm, golden).
- **Vocal:** warm, grinning **lead vocal** (male or female) with loose group harmonies on the chorus.
