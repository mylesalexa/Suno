# Forty Summers *(interview-with-a-local-surfer intro — warm nostalgic lo-fi)*

*Track 6 of **Salt & Static**. Opens on a short, warm interview snippet — someone asking an old local
surfer how long he's been coming to this beach, his gravelly answer, a soft laugh. Then a tribute song to
the guy who's outlasted every trend at the beach: same truck, same spot, forty summers running. Warm
cassette texture, acoustic-leaning. Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** every beach town has one — the guy who's been paddling out longer than most locals have been
alive, unbothered by condos or crowds. **The gimmick:** a real "man on the beach" interview snippet opens
the track, grounding the whole song as if it were inspired by an actual conversation.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **warm lo-fi acoustic surf-pop** — acoustic guitar with light tremolo electric accents, warm
cassette tape hiss, brushed drums. Nostalgic but not sad — proud, a little wry.

- **Tempo:** ~**92 BPM** (relaxed, warm).
- **Key:** **G major** (warm, nostalgic).
- **Vocal:** warm, storytelling **lead vocal** (male or female) — third-person narrator, fond and a little amused.
