# Home Movie *(retro surf-bum pop — grainy, warped, old-tape lo-fi)*

*The companion to **Salt Air** — same clean, almost-meaningless surf-bum lane, pushed further into a
**degraded old-tape aesthetic**: cassette hiss, vinyl crackle, warped wow-and-flutter pitch, muffled
AM-radio mono quality — like the audio is bleeding through a Super-8 home movie of a July beach day. The
song even "rewinds" mid-track before snapping back in. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief:** another surf-bum track, same vibe as Salt Air, but add retro grainy/bad-recording texture —
> tape hiss, warble, degraded quality, like an old home movie.

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the song *is* the old tape — not a modern recording with a filter slapped on, but a track that
sounds like it's actually playing off a warped cassette of a childhood beach day. Grainy, warm, a little
broken. **The structural trick:** instead of Salt Air's tempo-drop float, this one **warps and "rewinds"**
mid-song — the pitch bends down like a dying tape motor, a rewind whir plays, and it snaps back into the
final chorus a little brighter, like someone just hit play again.
**Lyrics stay light on purpose** — same "almost meaningless" rule as Salt Air: no plot, just the *texture*
of a memory (sun-bleached colors, static where the ending got erased, sand in the camera).

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **lo-fi retro surf pop** — cassette tape hiss, vinyl crackle, warped wow-and-flutter pitch,
muffled AM-radio mono quality, reverb tremolo surf guitar, loose brushed drums. Warm, grainy, intentionally
degraded — the "bad recording" IS the production.

- **Tempo:** ~**98 BPM** (a touch slower and dreamier than Salt Air), with a **tape-warp/rewind effect**
  mid-song before snapping back to tempo.
- **Key:** **D major** (warm, bright, guitar-friendly).
- **Vocal:** breezy, **unpolished lead** (male or female) — slightly buried in the mix, warm and close-mic'd,
  loose harmonies. Clean, nostalgic, unbothered. (Duet/harmony swap noted.)

```
Lo-fi retro surf pop, 98 BPM, D major, cassette tape hiss, vinyl crackle, warped wow-and-flutter pitch, AM-radio mono quality, tremolo surf guitar, breezy unpolished vocal, grainy nostalgic
```
**Tip:** `cassette tape hiss` + `warped wow-and-flutter pitch` are the load-bearing phrases — without them
Suno will just make a clean surf-pop track. `AM-radio mono quality` pushes it further into "bad recording"
territory. If it's too degraded to enjoy → pull back to `light tape hiss, subtle warble`; if it's not grainy
enough → add `heavy vinyl crackle, distant muffled mix, VHS quality`. For the rewind effect specifically, add
`tape rewind sound effect, pitch bend down then snap back` if Suno doesn't catch it from the lyric tag alone.

## Variations
- **Female-lead / duet:** swap `breezy unpolished vocal` → `breezy female vocal`, or `male-female loose harmony trade-off`.
- **Even more degraded:** add `heavy tape saturation, wobbly pitch throughout, bedroom 4-track recording`.
- **Less degraded (cleaner nostalgia):** pull back to `warm vintage tone, light tape warmth` instead of full hiss/crackle.
- **Companion set:** pairs with `salt-air-retro-surf.md` — same lane, Salt Air is the morning paddle-out, this is the grainy home movie of the whole day.
