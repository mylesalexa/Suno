# Night Swim *(hushed, dreamy nocturnal surf — reverb-heavy, restrained)*

*The fifth surf-bum flavor, and the quietest — the nighttime bookend to **Salt Air**'s morning energy.
Heavy shimmering reverb, brushed slow drums, warm pads, a hushed close vocal. **The gimmick here is
restraint:** no big chorus payoff, no loud final section — it stays quiet the whole way through, on purpose,
because it's about floating in dark water under stars, not paddling out for a wave. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief:** part of the "mix up the vibes" batch — this one goes nocturnal and hushed, the opposite energy
> of a normal "build to a big chorus" song.

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** floating on your back in dark water at night — phosphorescent glints, stars, a distant dying
bonfire on the shore, everyone quiet. **The structural choice:** most songs build to something bigger; this
one deliberately doesn't. It stays hushed and unresolved, because that's true to the feeling — you don't
want the moment to get *bigger*, you want it to stay exactly this quiet.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **hushed dreamy surf ballad** — heavy shimmering reverb, brushed slow drums, warm analog pads,
light tremolo guitar. Restrained the entire way through — no drop, no big final chorus, just a slow warm
swell at the very end and then it settles back down.

- **Tempo:** ~**68 BPM** (slow, floating, unhurried).
- **Key:** **E minor** (moody but warm — nocturnal, not sad).
- **Vocal:** hushed, intimate **lead vocal** (male or female) — close, breathy, almost whispered on the
  verses, with soft wordless "ooh" harmonies. Restrained even at its warmest. (Duet/harmony swap noted.)

## ⭐ Best Suno prompt
```
Hushed dreamy surf ballad, 68 BPM, E minor, heavy shimmering reverb, brushed slow drums, warm analog pads, light tremolo guitar, whispered intimate vocal, restrained, nocturnal, no big drop
```
**Tip:** `restrained` and `no big drop` are doing real work in this prompt — without them Suno will likely
try to build this into a normal pop chorus. If it still gets too big, add `stays quiet throughout, minimal
dynamics` explicitly. If it's TOO static/boring, allow just the one `warm swell` moment back in rather than
cutting all dynamics.

## Variations
- **Slightly more lift:** if fully restrained doesn't render well, allow `soft drums enter on final chorus only` for a touch more movement.
- **Male-female duet:** add `male-female whispered harmony` on the verses.
- **More ambient:** add `long reverb tails, ambient pad wash, minimal percussion` and let it drift further from "song" toward "soundscape."
