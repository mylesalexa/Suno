# Not on Any Map *(interview-with-a-customer intro — warm community lo-fi anthem)*

*Track 8 of **Salt & Static**. Opens on a shop customer being asked why they keep coming back — a warm,
half-laughing answer about a town so small it's basically a secret. Then a proud, communal anthem about the
hidden-gem beach town itself: one stoplight, a diner that hasn't changed, everybody's somebody's cousin.
Gang vocals, handclaps, warm community-radio texture. Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** not a person this time, but the *town* — small, unmarked, word-of-mouth only. **The gimmick:**
a real customer-interview snippet opens the track, so the anthem feels earned rather than just asserted.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **warm lo-fi community surf-pop anthem** — warm jangly guitar, handclaps, gang vocal chants,
brushed drums, community-radio/vinyl warmth. Proud, communal, upbeat.

- **Tempo:** ~**104 BPM** (upbeat, communal).
- **Key:** **A major** (bright, anthemic).
- **Vocal:** warm **lead vocal** (male or female) with **gang vocal** backing — this one's meant to be sung
  by a group, not just one voice.
