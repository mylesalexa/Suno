# Open Til the Swell Comes In *(retro surf-bum pop — life inside the surf shop)*

*The sixth surf-bum flavor — this one stays on land, behind the counter. A day in the life of a surf shop:
wax on the counter, boards on the rack, the regular who never buys anything, the tourist who can't pick a
board, a dog asleep in the sun, and a radio crackling the surf report. **The gimmick:** the song ends
mid-word — the swell comes in, everyone bolts, the door slams, and the track just cuts off. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief:** part of the surf-bum folder — this one's lyrics are about life in the surf shop specifically,
> not the water or the beach.

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the surf shop as its own little world — the smell of wax, the bell over the door, the same
regulars every day, a shop dog who's seen it all. Still light and breezy, but this one actually has
characters and a setting instead of pure vibes. **The payoff line — and the whole shop's philosophy — is
the title:** the sign says "open," but everybody who works there knows the real hours are dictated by the
surf report, not the clock. **The gimmick:** the song doesn't fade out — the swell report crackles in,
everyone drops what they're doing, and the track cuts off mid-word as the door slams.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **retro surf-bum pop** — warm jangly guitar (less reverb-drenched than the water-themed cuts,
more "shop radio" than "ocean"), door chime bell, brushed drums, warm bass, a crackling AM-radio surf-report
texture running through the background. Breezy, character-driven, still clean and unhurried.

- **Tempo:** ~**100 BPM** (relaxed shop-day groove).
- **Key:** **G major** (bright, warm, easygoing).
- **Vocal:** breezy, conversational **lead vocal** (male or female) — almost telling a story to a friend,
  warm and easy on the chorus. Clean, funny without trying too hard. (Duet/harmony swap noted.)
