# Shop Dog (Reef) *(whimsical spotlight track — bright, bouncy, playful lo-fi)*

*Track 7 of **Salt & Static**. The shop dog's been in the background of two other songs — asleep in a
patch of sun in both Salt Air and Open Til the Swell Comes In — and now he gets his own track. Bright,
bouncy, ukulele-driven, purely for fun. His name's Reef. Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the album's continuity paying off — a background detail (the dog) becomes his own spotlight
song. Pure whimsy, zero stakes, a deliberate light interlude right after the shop song and before the more
reflective Forty Summers.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **bright bouncy lo-fi surf-pop** — ukulele-led, playful, light percussion, warm tape hiss. Fun
and a little silly, but not childish.

- **Tempo:** ~**108 BPM** (bouncy, upbeat).
- **Key:** **C major** (bright, cheerful).
- **Vocal:** playful, grinning **lead vocal** (male or female), plus a barking dog as a recurring ad-lib.
