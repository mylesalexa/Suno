# Small Craft Advisory *(garbled AM beach-conditions report intro — upbeat lo-fi)*

*Track 3 of **Salt & Static**. Opens on a garbled, low-quality AM radio marine forecast — the classic
"waist to chest high, light onshore wind" beach report — then breaks into an upbeat song about obsessively
checking that same report every morning and chasing whatever it says. Warm jangly guitar, radio-garble
texture throughout, playful and bright. Cloudlight Proxy.*

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** the daily ritual of checking the surf report, played for its own low-stakes comedy — calling
in "sick," trusting a crackly 25-year-old radio over your own eyes. **The gimmick:** a genuine low-quality
marine-forecast broadcast opens the track (and briefly returns mid-song), so the whole thing is framed as
something you'd hear tuning across the dial early in the morning.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **lo-fi retro surf-bum pop** — warm jangly guitar, garbled AM-radio texture, brushed drums,
handclaps, upbeat and bright. Playful, a little goofy, still clean.

- **Tempo:** ~**112 BPM** (upbeat, chipper).
- **Key:** **E major** (bright, fun).
- **Vocal:** breezy, excitable **lead vocal** (male or female) — a little too enthusiastic for how early it is.
