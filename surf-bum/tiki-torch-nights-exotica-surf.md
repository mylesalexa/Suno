# Tiki Torch Nights *(retro tiki-lounge exotica — slow, warm, cocktail-hour surf-adjacent)*

*A fourth surf-bum flavor — this one trades the water for the **boardwalk bar at sunset**: vintage tiki-
lounge exotica (vibraphone, marimba, congas), warm and unhurried, cocktail-hour instead of dawn-patrol
energy. Still clean, still almost meaningless — just pineapple, torches, and nowhere to be. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief:** part of the "mix up the vibes" batch — this one leans lounge/exotica, slower and warmer,
> evening instead of morning.

---

## Concept & Angle
**Concept:** if Salt Air is the morning paddle-out, this is the evening after — torches lit, feet in the
sand, a slow marimba melody drifting over the boardwalk bar. Still no plot, still no lesson — just warm
imagery of a beach evening winding down. **The texture shift:** vibraphone and marimba replace the guitar as
the lead voice; congas and bongos replace the surf-beat drums. A different instrumental world entirely.

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **retro tiki-lounge exotica** — vibraphone and marimba lead melody, congas and bongos, upright
bass, warm brushed drums, light tremolo guitar accents. Slow, warm, unhurried — cocktail-hour, not
adrenaline. Ambient night sounds (crickets, distant surf, torch crackle) throughout.

- **Tempo:** ~**86 BPM** (slow, swaying, lounge pace).
- **Key:** **F major** (warm, mellow, lounge-friendly).
- **Vocal:** relaxed, breezy **lead vocal** (male or female) — soft, close-mic'd, almost a lullaby delivery;
  light scat "la-la" or "doo" vocals in the instrumental sections. Clean, warm, unhurried. (Instrumental-only version noted.)
