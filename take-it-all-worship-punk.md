# Take It All

*Christian worship pop-punk — the surrender/adoration track. The record's first purely vertical moment:
after all the testimony and the altar call, this is the response — hands open, everything laid down,
eyes on Jesus alone. Builds from intimate confession to a huge communal worship explosion. This is the
one where we stop talking about it and just adore Him.*

---

## Creative Direction

**Topic:** *Total surrender and adoration — "take it all, You're worthy of my whole life."* It's the
natural sequel to the altar call ("Off the Fence"): you came off the fence — now here is everything. The
first song on the record that turns fully vertical, from *our story* to *His worth*.

**Why it works:** the album has been honest, urgent, and outward-facing. It needs one moment that simply
kneels. Starting intimate (one voice, open hands) and building to a whole-room "we will worship" gives the
listener the catharsis of letting go — and it's the truest response to grace: not a transaction, an offering.

**Bible verses:**
1. **Romans 12:1** — "offer your bodies as a living sacrifice... this is your true and proper worship." *(the chorus)*
2. **Galatians 2:20** — "I no longer live, but Christ lives in me." *(verse 2)*
3. **Philippians 3:8** — "I consider everything a loss... that I may gain Christ." *(pre-chorus)*
4. **Revelation 5:12** — "Worthy is the Lamb... to receive power and wealth and wisdom." *(the hook — "You're worthy")*
5. **Psalm 73:25** — "Whom have I in heaven but you? And earth has nothing I desire besides you." *(verse 2)*
6. **Revelation 4:8** — "Holy, holy, holy is the Lord God Almighty." *(the bridge)*
7. **Luke 9:23** — "whoever wants to be my disciple must... take up their cross and follow me." *(surrender)*

**Emotional arc:** clutching the little throne I built (V1) → "I'd lose it all to gain just You" (pre) →
the chorus opens — hands open, take it all, You're worthy (chorus) → "it's not me that lives, it's You in
me" (V2) → "take the pen, the plan, the wheel" (pre 2) → the bridge erupts into communal adoration: "Holy,
holy, holy — worthy of it all" (bridge) → full final chorus → bare, surrendered outro: "it's all Yours."

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal

**Genre:** emotive worship pop-punk that builds intimate-to-huge. Soft clean/arpeggiated verses, a wide
soaring adoring chorus, and a bridge that becomes a full-room worship moment. Dynamic, hands-up, devotional.

- **Tempo:** ~**136–142 BPM**, dynamic — verses pulled back, chorus and bridge full.
- **Key:** **C** major, lift to **D** for the bridge/final chorus.
- **Vocal:** earnest, warm male lead — intimate and surrendered in the verses, full and soaring in the
  chorus. The bridge brings layered communal/gang worship vocals ("we will worship," "holy") — the one
  place the whole room sings together.
