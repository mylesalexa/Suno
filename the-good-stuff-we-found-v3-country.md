## Style / Tempo / Vocal

**Genre:** 90s country ballad — warm, organic, radio-classic. Acoustic guitar, fiddle, weeping pedal
steel, brushed drums, upright/electric bass; lush harmonies on the choruses. Live-band, front-porch honesty.

- **Tempo:** ~**80–84 BPM** — slow-to-mid, gentle sway.
- **Key:** **D major**, optional lift to **E** for the final chorus.
- **Vocal:** **female lead** — warm, clear, sentimental, light twang; tender in verses, full on the hook.
  Sweet harmonies on "the good stuff we found." Clean, **no autotune**.

## Full Lyrics

### Verse 1
Got a suitcase full of memories, a map with worn-out lines,
Chasing sunsets through old cities, making the most of borrowed time.
From cobblestone streets to mountain peaks, we've wandered far and wide,
But my favorite destination's always sitting by your side.

### Pre-Chorus
Every road keeps leading somewhere, every mile tells a tale,
But the best parts of this journey ain't the places on the trail.

### Chorus
It's red wine on the back deck, talking long after dark,
Just me and you and a million stars hanging over where we are.
It's campfire smoke and old friends' jokes, while the kids run wild and free,
Their laughter floating through the pines like a sweet old melody.
It's all these little moments that money just can't buy —
The good stuff we found along the ride.

### Verse 2
I still see those Disney lights shining bright against the night,
Walking down Main Street together, watching wonder fill their eyes.
That castle standing in the distance, like a dream we'd finally found,
And for a minute we were kids again, spinning circles all around.

### Chorus
It's red wine on the back deck, talking long after dark,
Just me and you and a million stars hanging over where we are.
It's campfire smoke and old friends' jokes, while the kids run wild and free,
Their laughter floating through the pines like a sweet old melody.
It's all these little moments that money just can't buy —
The good stuff we found along the ride.

### Bridge
*(soften, then build)*
Harper's got a laugh that could wake up the moon,
Comes rolling through the campsite like a favorite tune.
And Charley's summer freckles show up right on cue,
Like little drops of sunshine painted fresh and new.
And Teddy chases after 'em with that goldendoodle grin,
Floppy ears and sandy paws, our happy little friend.
I've watched them climb mountains that looked way too high,
Digging deep for courage with determination in their eyes.
And every step they take reminds me time won't stand still,
So I'm holding onto every memory I can fill.

### Final Chorus
It's evening walks with Mark, talking softly hand in hand,
Watching daylight fade away across this promised land.
It's passports, hiking trails, and all the places left to see,
But home is every moment when you're right here next to me.
Yeah, life's not in the headlines or the dreams we're chasing by —
It's the good stuff we found along the ride.

### Outro
*(soft, warm, slowing)*
A world full of adventures, and we've only just begun,
Counting every blessing underneath the setting sun.
If tomorrow looks like today, I'll be satisfied —
With the good stuff we found along the ride.
