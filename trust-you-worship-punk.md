# I Don't Know (But You Do)

*Christian worship pop-punk — the forward-motion anthem of the set. Where "Anchor" holds on and "Still"
exhales, this one walks: surrendering an unknown future to Jesus and stepping into it anyway. Driving,
hopeful, eyes-up Good Charlotte energy with a big gang-vocal singalong.*

---

## Creative Direction

**Topic:** *Trusting Jesus with an unknown future — walking by faith into what you can't see.* The whole
song is the prayer "I don't have the map, but I trust the One who does." It's surrender as forward motion,
not paralysis — which is why the music drives instead of sitting still.

**Why it works:** uncertainty about the future is the most universal modern anxiety (career, health,
relationships, the state of the world). Naming that honestly in the verses and answering it with
white-knuckle-letting-go in the chorus is deeply relatable *and* deeply biblical. It's hopeful without
being naïve — it admits "I don't know" and trusts anyway. Easy to shout, easy to mean.

**Bible verses:**
1. **Proverbs 3:5–6** — "Trust in the Lord with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding... and he will make your paths straight." *(the chorus)*
2. **Hebrews 11:8** — Abraham "obeyed and went, even though he did not know where he was going." *(verse 2)*
3. **2 Corinthians 5:7** — "We walk by faith, not by sight." *(the chorus)*
4. **Jeremiah 29:11** — "I know the plans I have for you... plans to give you hope and a future." *("You already wrote it")*
5. **Psalm 119:105** — "Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path." *(verse 2)*
6. **Matthew 6:34** — "Do not worry about tomorrow, for tomorrow will worry about itself." *(the bridge)*
7. **Deuteronomy 31:8** — "The Lord himself goes before you and will be with you." *(the bridge)*
8. **Joshua 1:9** — "Be strong and courageous... the Lord your God will be with you wherever you go."

**Emotional arc:** lost at the crossroad, gripping for control (V1) → surrender, handing over the wheel
(pre-chorus) → declaration of trust in the unknown (chorus) → Abraham-faith, the Word as light (V2) →
"He goes before me, I won't borrow tomorrow's weight" (bridge) → bigger committed final chorus → peaceful
resolve: *"I don't know what's ahead, but You already know"* (outro).

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal

**Genre:** anthemic Christian pop-punk worship — driving and hopeful, between "Anchor's" aggression and
"Still's" melody. Palm-muted verses opening to wide open-chord choruses, ringing octave lead line over the
hook, punchy punk drums, big gang-vocal "whoa-oh"s.

- **Tempo:** ~**158–162 BPM** — bright and bouncy, four-on-the-floor pop-punk, radio-single energy.
- **Key:** **E** major, lift to **F#** for the final chorus.
- **Vocal:** young, hopeful, full-chested male lead — earnest in the verses, soaring and committed in the
  chorus. Gang vocals on the "I don't know, but You do" call-and-response and the outro.
