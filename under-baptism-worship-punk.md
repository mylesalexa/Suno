# Under (Came Up New)

*Christian worship pop-punk — companion heart-wrench to "Empty." Same raw, dynamic deep-cut style: a
testimony of the pivotal moment in the baptism waters — going under with the weight of the old life,
breaking the surface completely changed. Buried with Him, raised with Him. Intimate verses that detonate
into a cathartic chorus.*

---

## Creative Direction

**Topic:** *The supernatural transition of baptism — you go under one person and come up another.* The
song lives entirely in that moment: the held breath, the water closing over the old self, the gasp of new
air, and the inexplicable lightness of coming up changed. It's the natural sequel to "Empty" — that song
was *being found*; this one is *being buried and raised*.

**Why it works:** baptism is the most cinematic moment in a believer's story — a literal death and
resurrection you can *feel*. Grounding the lyrics in real testimony language (the weight lifting, colors
turning brighter, a feeling you can't explain) keeps it from being abstract; it puts the listener under
the water. And the honest tension in the bridge — *"you can call it just water, but I came up not the
same"* — is what makes it grip instead of preach.

**Bible verses:**
1. **Romans 6:3–4** — "buried with him through baptism into death... so we too may walk in newness of life." *(the entire song — the going under and coming up)*
2. **Colossians 2:12** — "having been buried with him in baptism... you were also raised with him through your faith."
3. **2 Corinthians 5:17** — "if anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: the old has gone." *(the chorus)*
4. **Acts 22:16** — "be baptized and wash your sins away." *(verse 1)*
5. **1 Peter 3:21** — "baptism that now saves you — not the removal of dirt from the body, but the pledge of a clear conscience." *(the bridge — "you can call it just water")*
6. **Mark 1:10–11** — "heaven being torn open... a voice came: 'You are my beloved.'" *(the bridge — "tore the sky open, called me by my name")*
7. **Titus 3:5** — "he saved us through the washing of rebirth and renewal by the Holy Spirit."
8. **Acts 2:38** — "Repent and be baptized... and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit."

**Emotional arc:** the walk to the water with a lifetime on his back (V1) → the held breath, the let-go
(pre-chorus) → going under / breaking the surface new (chorus) → the supernatural aftermath, weight gone,
the world brighter (V2) → the bridge confession: *"I went down one man and came up not the same"* → full
cathartic final chorus → bare, broken outro: *"I went down a dyin' man, and I came up with You."*

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal

**Genre:** raw, dynamic pop-punk — same emotional deep-cut family as "Empty." Intimate clean/arpeggiated
guitar verses (bass + ghosted drums), exploding into a driving, distorted, cathartic chorus. Big dynamic
swings; a single raw lead carries it, group vocals only as soft echoes at the very end.

- **Tempo:** ~**148–152 BPM**, with deep dynamic dips (verses pulled back, chorus full and crashing).
- **Key:** **C** major with **minor-leaning verses** resolving up into the chorus (the shadow-to-light lift
  doubles as the under-to-surface motion).
- **Vocal:** gritty, vulnerable, *cracking* male lead — hushed and close-mic'd in the verses, full-throated
  and a little broken in the chorus. Keep it human and unpolished. Soft layered echoes on the outro only.

## Full Lyrics

### Verse 1
*(quiet, intimate, raw)*
I walked down to the water with a lifetime on my back,
Every name they ever called me, every road I can't take back.
Felt his hand behind my shoulder, heard the hush fall on the crowd,
He said, "buried with Him, raised with Him" — and he laid me down.

### Pre-Chorus
*(building)*
So I held my breath and I let it all go,
Felt the water close over the man I used to know —

### Chorus
*(full band, cathartic)*
I went under with the weight of everything I'd been,
Let it bury me, let it bury all my sin.
Then I broke the surface gaspin' on a brand new kind of air,
The old me stayed down under — I left him right there.
I came up, I came up — alive for the very first time,
Washed in the water, and the old me died.

### Verse 2
*(back down, raw)*
I came up and the whole world never looked so loud and bright,
Like the color got turned up and the gray bled into light.
I can't tell you what I felt down there beneath the wave,
But the part of me that drowned is the part I couldn't save.

### Pre-Chorus
*(building)*
And the weight I'd carried years just lifted off my chest,
Came up so light I could barely catch my breath —

### Chorus
*(full band, cathartic)*
I went under with the weight of everything I'd been,
Let it bury me, let it bury all my sin.
Then I broke the surface gaspin' on a brand new kind of air,
The old me stayed down under — I left him right there.
I came up, I came up — alive for the very first time,
Washed in the water, and the old me died.

### Bridge
*(strip it bare — just voice, then build)*
You can call it only water, you can call it just a name,
But I went down one man and I came up not the same.
Somethin' tore the sky wide open, somethin' called me by my name —
And I'll spend my whole life tryin' to say what it all meant.
*(building)*
I came up... I came up...
*(full band slams back in)*

### Final Chorus
I went under with the weight of everything I'd been,
Let it bury me, let it bury all my sin.
Then I broke the surface gaspin' on a brand new kind of air,
The old me stayed down under — I left him right there.
I came up, I came up — alive for the very first time,
Washed in the water, and the old me died.

### Outro
*(bare, intimate, soft echoes)*
The old me stayed under *(stayed under)*,
I came up new —
I went down a dyin' man,
And I came up with You.
