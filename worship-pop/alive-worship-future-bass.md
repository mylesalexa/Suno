# Alive *(clean worship future-bass hip-hop — resurrection banger)*

*A worship banger in the melodic **future-bass hip-hop** lane (the Golden Moments style): melodic sung-rap
verses on a 150-half-time bounce that explode into a huge **festival EDM drop** — supersaw leads, lush
stretched chords, sidechain pump, deep sub. The message: **dead in the dark, God spoke, and I came alive** —
resurrection power, made new, all things new. The drop IS the moment life floods in. Clean, euphoric, hands-up. Cloudlight Proxy.*

## Creative Direction (topic · why it works · scripture · arc)
**Topic:** the resurrection moment — going from numb/dead-inside to fully alive when God speaks your name.
**Why it works:** the future-bass **build-and-drop** is *literally* the story — the riser is the tomb, the
drop is "come alive." Euphoria with a reason. Grace, not guilt; testimony, not sermon.

**Bible verses (where each lands):**
- **Ephesians 2:4–5** — "But God... made us alive together with Christ." → the **hook** ("You made me alive").
- **2 Corinthians 5:17** — "new creation; the old has gone, the new is here." → **hook** ("all things new").
- **John 11 (Lazarus)** — called out of the grave, the stone rolled away. → **hook** ("called me out the grave").
- **Ezekiel 37** — "dry bones, hear the word of the Lord" and live. → the **build** ("dry bones, hear the sound").
- **Galatians 2:20** — "crucified with Christ... Christ lives in me." → **Verse 2**.
- **Romans 8:11** — "the same Spirit that raised Jesus... lives in you." → **Verse 2** ("same power").
- **John 8:36** — "if the Son sets you free, you are free indeed." → the **bridge**.

**Emotional arc:** *numb/dead → He speaks → the drop, alive → I'm not the same → free indeed.*

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** clean **worship future-bass / hip-hop** — melodic sung-rap verses on a **150 BPM half-time**
bounce exploding into a **festival EDM drop** (supersaw leads, lush stretched chords, sidechain pump, deep
sub bass); huge sung hook, congregation/vocal-chop gang vocals. Euphoric, hands-up, radio-and-festival polished.

- **Tempo:** ~**150 BPM half-time** — verses bounce like hip-hop (~75 feel), the drop hits full future-bass.
- **Key:** **A minor** (emotional, euphoric lift on the drop).
- **Vocal:** confident **male** — melodic sung-rap on verses (tuneful, lots of pitch movement), big and
  euphoric on the drop-hook, stacked congregation + vocal-chop gang vocals. Light auto-tune fine. Clean. (Female/duet noted.)
