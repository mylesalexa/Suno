# Every Good Gift *(Christian worship dance-pop / EDM — a summer gratitude anthem)*

*An upbeat, hands-up **worship dance-pop / EDM** summer anthem about being **thankful to God for the little
things** — morning sunshine, breath, the ordinary blessings we take for granted — always coming back to
gratitude to Him for it all. Built on **James 1:17** ("every good and perfect gift is from above, from the
Father of lights") with a festival drop and big congregation vocals. In the Hillsong Young & Free / Elevation
EDM-worship lane — celebratory, clean, faith-filled. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief:** same summer dance-pop vibe as the last one, a touch **more EDM**, turned into **worship** —
> thankful for the little things (sunshine in the morning, all we take for granted), always returning to
> thankfulness to God. Publish-ready. New `worship-pop/` folder (separate from the pop-punk worship album).

---

## Creative Direction (topic · why it works · scripture · arc)
**Topic:** gratitude for everyday grace — the sun through the blinds, the breath, the coffee-quiet morning —
recognizing every good thing as a gift from God, and turning that into praise. **Why it works:** it's the
most *relatable* worship there is (everyone has ordinary mornings) fused to a **euphoric EDM drop**, so
gratitude becomes a celebration you dance to, not a duty.

**Bible verses (where each lands):**
- **James 1:17** — "Every good and perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of the heavenly
  lights." → the **chorus hook** ("every good gift comes down from You / Father of the lights").
- **Lamentations 3:22–23** — "His mercies... are new every morning." → **Verse 1 & 2** ("mercy's new, I feel it break of day").
- **1 Thessalonians 5:18** — "give thanks in all circumstances." → the **bridge** ("in everything I'll give You thanks").
- **Colossians 3:17** — "whatever you do... giving thanks to God." → the posture of the whole song.

**Emotional arc:** *takes it for granted → notices → lifts it up → all-out thankful praise.*

---

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **Christian worship dance-pop / EDM** — bright pop synths, four-on-the-floor, a festival **build
and drop**, claps, warm bass; **passionate worship lead vocal** into a huge sung hook with **congregation
gang vocals**. Hands-up, euphoric, radio-and-youth-service polished. Clean, faith-filled.

- **Tempo:** ~**122 BPM** — dance four-on-the-floor with an EDM lift on the chorus.
- **Key:** **C major** (bright, celebratory, morning-light).
- **Vocal:** passionate **worship lead** (male or female), warm and building on verses, soaring on the hook,
  big stacked **congregation/gang vocals** on the drop. Light auto-tune fine. Clean. (Male-female trade / full band noted.)

Young-&-Free → add `youth worship, bright topline, four-on-floor`; more emotional → add `key change final
chorus, big reverb`; harder EDM → add `soaring supersaw drop, sidechain pump`.

## Variations
- **Female-lead / duet:** swap `worship vocal` → `female worship vocal`, or `male-female trade-off`.
- **More EDM / festival:** add `soaring supersaw lead, white-noise riser, big drop`.
- **Acoustic-to-anthem build:** start V1 `(acoustic guitar, intimate)`, slam full band + drop at chorus 1.
- **Fuller band worship:** add `live drums, electric guitars, arena worship` for an Elevation-style build.
