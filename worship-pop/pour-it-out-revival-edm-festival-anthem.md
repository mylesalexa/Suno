## Researching the PRAANA × Tritonal × Arty "Kate" lane
*(artist/track names are filtered in Suno, so these become sound descriptors, not names)*
- **PRAANA** (Tritonal's melodic side project) → **deep, introspective trance**: warm, bubbly synth textures,
  pensive melodic pads, a mindful/clarity-and-healing atmosphere — softer and more subtle than a straight
  festival track. Perfect for verses that are honest and aching before they declare anything.
- **Tritonal** (the main project) → **festival progressive house / future bass**: big, emotional,
  vocal-driven **drops**, soaring supersaw leads, hard sidechain pump — pop and trance blended for both
  festival and radio.
- **Arty's "Kate"** (Anjunabeats progressive-trance classic) → packed with synths, percussion, and energy,
  built on **old-school Avicii-style piano and chord progressions** — a raw, melodic, driving piano lead
  carrying the drop rather than a vocal hook alone.
- **The fusion, plainly:** PRAANA's introspective warmth carries the verses and the pulled-back breakdown;
  Tritonal's festival drop carries the chorus and the final bridge; Arty's driving piano-chord lead rides
  underneath "pour it out, pour it out" on the drop, giving the vocal hook a melodic engine instead of leaving
  it to carry the drop alone.

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **melodic progressive house / trance-future-bass** — introspective, warm bubbly verses (PRAANA
lane) exploding into a huge **festival drop** (Tritonal lane): soaring supersaw leads, lush emotive chords,
sidechain pump, hard sub bass, and a **driving melodic piano-chord lead** (Arty "Kate" lane) carrying the
vocal hook through the drop. Hands-up, euphoric, clean.

- **Tempo:** ~**128 BPM** (progressive house / trance festival standard — verses can breathe half-time-feel).
- **Key:** **A minor** (emotional-euphoric, festival-anthemic, matches the rest of this catalog's Tritonal-lane tracks).
- **Vocal:** passionate lead (male or female) — intimate, almost spoken-sung on the verses, big and euphoric
  on the drop, with **huge gang/congregation vocals** doubling the hook. Clean, sincere, festival-scale.

## ⭐ Best Suno prompt
```
Melodic progressive house trance, 128 BPM, A minor, warm introspective pads, soaring supersaw leads, driving piano chords, sidechain pump, huge festival drop, euphoric build, congregation gang vocals
```
**Tip:** the fusion lives in three phrases — `warm introspective pads` (the PRAANA half, carries the verses
and breakdown), `soaring supersaw leads, sidechain pump` (the Tritonal half, carries the drop), and `driving
piano chords` (the Arty "Kate" half, gives the drop a melodic engine under the vocal hook) — keep all three
up front. Don't over-write the hook; `pour it out, pour it out` staying exactly four words is what lets the
piano lead and the supersaws carry the size instead of the lyric. If the drop isn't euphoric enough → add
`lush emotive chords, big festival build`; if the piano lead is too buried → add `prominent piano lead, raw
melodic chords`; if the verses lose the introspective feel → add `mindful pads, breathy vocal, half-time`;
harder → add `bigger sub bass, wobble drop`.

## Variations
- **Female-lead / duet:** the passionate lead works either way; try `confident female vocal` or `male-female trade-off` on the verses vs. drop.
- **More PRAANA/introspective:** add `deep melodic trance, lush atmospheric breakdown, healing pads` and pull the drop back slightly.
- **More Tritonal/festival:** add `bigger supersaw stack, harder sidechain, festival mainstage`.
- **More Arty/piano-trance:** add `raw melodic piano lead, classic progressive trance, driving chord stabs` and let the piano dominate the drop over the supersaws.
