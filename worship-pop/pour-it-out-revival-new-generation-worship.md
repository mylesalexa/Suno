# Pour It Out (Revival)

*Christian worship anthem — a revival prayer for a new generation that traces the whole arc of Scripture:
**prophesied** in Joel, **fulfilled** at Pentecost, and **consummated** in the throne room of Revelation.
Modern, youth-driven, anthemic worship-rock — built with the same full biblical backing as the Truth Has a
Name album and the Still Cove songs.*

---

## 1. Recommended Christian Topic

**A prayer for revival that rises through a new generation until it breaks into the eternal worship of
Revelation — "I will pour out my Spirit... your sons and daughters will prophesy" (Joel 2:28), fulfilled at
Pentecost (Acts 2), until "every knee will bow" before the Lamb (Revelation 5, 7).**

It's not nostalgia for a past move of God — it's one continuous story: promised, poured out, passed down,
and finished at the throne. "Revival" here means the whole arc, not just a nostalgic ask for "the good old days."

## 2. Why This Topic Works

- **It's the full biblical timeline, not just a mood.** Revival gets prophesied (Joel), fulfilled in history
  (Acts 2), lived out generation to generation (Psalm 145:4), and consummated in eternity (Revelation) — the
  bridge has somewhere massive and true to *go*, not just louder repetition.
- **"New generation" is scripturally exact, not just a marketing word.** Joel 2:28 specifically names sons,
  daughters, old men, young men — revival in Scripture has always been a generational, not individual, promise.
- **It's honest before it's hype.** Verse 1 names the ache — the noise, the hunger for something real —
  before the chorus declares the promise. That keeps it from feeling like empty adrenaline.
- **It ends somewhere no other song in this catalog goes.** The final bridge lands in Revelation's actual
  throne-room scene — "every knee," "every nation, tribe, and tongue," "holy, holy, holy" — the eternal
  worship revival is pointing toward the whole time.

## 3. Bible Verses Supporting the Theme

1. **Joel 2:28** — "I will pour out my Spirit on all people. Your sons and daughters will prophesy, your old
   men will dream dreams, your young men will see visions." *(the chorus's central promise.)*
2. **Acts 2:17–18** — Peter, at Pentecost, quoting Joel: "In the last days, God says, I will pour out my
   Spirit on all people." *(the historical fulfillment — Pentecost as revival already begun.)*
3. **Psalm 145:4** — "One generation will commend Your works to another; they will tell of Your mighty acts."
   *(Verse 2 — the torch passed down, prayers still echoing.)*
4. **2 Chronicles 7:14** — "If My people... will humble themselves and pray... I will hear from heaven and
   will heal their land." *(the bridge's opening — humility before the ask.)*
5. **Habakkuk 3:2** — "Lord, I have heard of Your fame... Renew them in our day, in our time make them known."
   *(the bridge's direct revival cry — "renew Your works... in our day.")*
6. **Revelation 5:12** — "Worthy is the Lamb, who was slain, to receive power and wealth and wisdom and
   might and honor and glory and praise!" *(the final bridge's declaration.)*
7. **Revelation 7:9–10** — a multitude from every nation, tribe, people, and language, crying out before the
   throne. *(the final bridge — "every nation, every tribe, every tongue.")*
8. **Revelation 4:8** — "Holy, holy, holy is the Lord God Almighty." *(the outro's closing tag — eternal
   worship, still going.)*

**Tempo & vocal feel:** ~**130 BPM with a half-time feel** (the modern-worship trick: verses breathe at a
half-time ~65 BPM feel, the chorus and bridge hit at full tempo). Key of **E** (lift for the final chorus).
Lead vocal: youthful, urgent, building from restrained to full-throated by the bridge; huge stacked
congregation/gang vocals on the chorus and especially the final bridge.
