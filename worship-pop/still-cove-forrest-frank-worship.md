# Still Cove *(Forrest-Frank-style worship gospel-pop — a thankful family-cabin anthem)*

*A warm, bouncy **worship gospel-pop** anthem about **Still Cove** — the family cabin place. Golden-hue
summer sunsets, boating out into the gold, kneeboarding behind the boat, a dock full of people so you never
feel short a seat, the odd holler of the neighbors having a good time somewhere down the shore, jumping in
the water, and every bit of it turning into praise. Built in the **Forrest Frank** lane — beat-driven,
laid-back, joyful gospel-pop-hip-hop (think "Good Day" / "UP!"): bouncy groove, warm chords, claps, a
feel-good hook you'd catch yourself singing on a Tuesday, unapologetically thankful to God. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief:** a new worship song, Forrest-Frank style (researched below), about **Still Cove**, the family
> cabin. Golden summer sunsets, boating into the sunset, kneeboarding, enough people around to feel like you
> belong, the odd holler of the neighbors having a good time in the distance, swimming, and praising and
> thanking God for each day.

## Researching the Forrest Frank lane
*(artist name is filtered in Suno, so this becomes sound descriptors, not a name)*
- **Sound:** beat-driven gospel-pop that blends hip-hop, lo-fi pop, R&B, and soul — bouncy, danceable
  grooves under a relaxed, conversational vocal delivery. Not a big EDM drop (that's the *Every Good Gift*
  lane) — this is **groove-first, feel-good, mid-tempo**.
- **Vibe:** vibrant, sunny, "vibiest Christian music" — built to work as background music for real life
  (a road trip, a dock, a Tuesday) just as much as a worship set. Warm, not heavy; joyful, not somber.
- **Lyrics:** contemporary, plainspoken thankfulness and devotion — not hymn structure, more like talking to
  a friend who happens to be God. Repetition of a simple, huge-hearted hook over craft.
- **Translation for this song:** bouncy lo-fi gospel-pop-hip-hop groove, warm chords/keys, claps, laid-back
  joyful lead vocal, gang "thank You" backing vocals — sunshine you can nod your head to.

## Creative Direction (topic · why it works · scripture · arc)
**Topic:** a whole summer day at the family cabin — Still Cove — read as one long, ordinary miracle: the
boat, the kneeboard, the people, the sunset, the water — all of it a gift, and thankfulness the natural
response. **Why it works:** everyone has a "their place" like this; naming it (Still Cove) makes it personal
and specific while the gratitude is universal.

**Bible verses (where each lands):**
- **Psalm 118:24** — "This is the day the Lord has made; let us rejoice and be glad in it." → the **chorus
  hook** ("this is the day, this is the day You made").
- **1 Chronicles 16:34** — "Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good; His love endures forever." → the
  **bridge** ("give thanks, 'cause You are good").
- **Psalm 133:1** — "How good and pleasant it is when God's people live together in unity." → **Verse 1**
  (the full dock, always room for one more).
- **Psalm 19:1** — "The heavens declare the glory of God." → **Verse 2** (the golden-hue sunset).

**Emotional arc:** *arrive and unwind → soak in the people and the gold light → name it as a gift → praise
that carries past the weekend into every ordinary day.*

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **worship gospel-pop / hip-hop groove** — bouncy beat, warm chords and keys, claps, laid-back joyful
lead vocal, gang "thank You" backing vocals. Feel-good, sunny, mid-tempo — groove over drop. Clean,
faith-filled, radio-and-youth-service polished.

- **Tempo:** ~**96 BPM** (bouncy head-nod groove, not a festival four-on-the-floor).
- **Key:** **D major** (bright, warm, guitar/ukulele-friendly).
- **Vocal:** relaxed, joyful **lead vocal** (male or female) — conversational and warm on verses, big and
  sunny on the hook, with **gang "thank You" backing vocals** and claps. Light, unforced, sincere.
  (Male-female trade / full-band noted.)

## ⭐ Best Suno prompt
```
Warm gospel-pop hip-hop groove, 96 BPM, D major, bouncy beat, claps, warm chords and keys, laid-back joyful vocal, gang thank-you backing vocals, feel-good worship anthem, radio polished
```
**Tip:** the whole feel lives in `bouncy beat` + `laid-back joyful vocal` — keep the tempo groove-first, not
a festival drop (that's a different worship lane in this catalog). `gang thank-you backing vocals` is what
makes the chorus feel like a crowd, not a solo. If it's too laid-back → add `bright pop chorus, bigger claps`;
too plain → add `warm brass swells, gospel choir stabs`; too heavy → keep `EDM drop` and `four-on-the-floor`
in Exclude.

## Variations
- **Female-lead / duet:** swap `laid-back joyful vocal` → `laid-back joyful female vocal`, or `male-female trade-off`.
- **Fuller band / live worship:** add `live drums, acoustic guitar, warm Rhodes piano` for an unplugged-cabin-porch feel.
- **More gospel:** add `gospel choir, Hammond organ swells, tambourine`.
- **Bigger festival cousin:** this catalog's EDM-drop worship lane lives in `every-good-gift-worship-dance.md` — pair the two for a set that goes chill → huge.
- **Title/hook swaps:** "This Is the Day," "Golden on the Water," "Thank You, God," "Still Thankful."
