# Still Cove (Be Still) *(Hillsong-style modern arena worship anthem)*

*A genuine, big **modern arena worship** anthem about **Still Cove** — the family cabin — built around the
name itself as a scripture pun: **"Be still, and know that I am God" (Psalm 46:10)**. Same place as the
gospel-pop cut (golden sunsets, the boat, the full dock, the neighbors hollerin' down the shore) but told as
a soaring, cinematic **Hillsong-lane** worship anthem — intimate verses that build into a huge congregational
chorus and an extended, repeated declaration bridge. Soft-rock, string pads, soaring guitar swells, live-choir
scale. Cloudlight Proxy.*

## Researching the Hillsong lane
*(artist name is filtered in Suno, so this becomes sound descriptors, not a name)*
- **Sound:** modern praise & worship — CCM rooted in **pop, ambient pop, and soft-rock**; anthemic and
  emotional across the board. Not groove-first (that's the Forrest-Frank-lane cut) — this is **build-first**:
  intimate and sparse, then swelling into something huge.
- **Production:** the "sound" is live-room scale — recorded like an arena with a crowd, a **live
  choir/congregation**, soaring electric guitar swells, string pads, driving toms building under the chorus.
  Excellent, cinematic production married to passionately delivered, deeply personal vocals.
- **Songwriting signature:** an ordinary word or object turned into a **scripture-anchored metaphor**
  ("Oceans," "Anchor," "Touch the Sky") — simple, singable melodies any congregation can pick up, then an
  **extended repeated-declaration bridge** that keeps building rather than just repeating the chorus.
- **Translation for this song:** modern arena worship soft-rock, soaring guitar swells, string pads, driving
  toms, passionate lead vocal, huge congregational choir, anthemic build into a repeated bridge.

## Creative Direction (topic · why it works · scripture · arc)
**Topic:** "Still Cove" isn't just the cabin's name — it's the whole song's theology. The stillness of the
water becomes an invitation to **be still before God**, using **Psalm 46:10** as the spine. **Why it works:**
it's the "ordinary-word-becomes-metaphor" move Hillsong is built on — a real, personal place turned into
something any congregation can sing about their own "Still Cove."

**Bible verses (where each lands):**
- **Psalm 46:10** — "Be still, and know that I am God." → the **chorus hook**, and the title pun.
- **Psalm 65:8** — "You call forth songs of joy where morning dawns and evening fades." → **Verse 1**
  (the golden sunset fading over the water).
- **Psalm 133:1** — "How good and pleasant it is when God's people live together in unity." → **Verse 2**
  (the full dock, the neighbors down the shore).
- **Habakkuk 2:20** — "The Lord is in His holy temple; let all the earth be silent before Him." → the
  **bridge**, the big repeated declaration.

**Emotional arc:** *arrive and exhale → grow quiet in the beauty → name God as the reason for the stillness
→ an unrestrained, building declaration of trust → carry that stillness back into ordinary life.*

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** **modern arena worship / soft-rock** — soaring electric guitar swells, lush string pads, driving
toms building under the chorus, warm piano; **passionate lead vocal** into a huge **congregational choir**
chorus and an extended, building declaration bridge. Cinematic, emotional, live-room scale.

- **Tempo:** ~**74 BPM** (a building ballad-to-anthem — not a dance groove, not an EDM drop).
- **Key:** **A major** (bright, congregation-friendly, room to lift for the final chorus).
- **Vocal:** passionate, emotive **lead vocal** (male or female) — intimate and restrained on verses, soaring
  and full on the chorus, with a **huge congregational choir** joining by the bridge. Sincere, unforced, big
  dynamic range. (Male-female trade / worship-leader-and-congregation call-and-response noted.)

## ⭐ Best Suno prompt
```
Modern arena worship soft-rock, 74 BPM, A major, soaring electric guitar swells, string pads, driving toms, passionate lead vocal, huge congregational choir, anthemic build, repeated bridge
```
**Tip:** the magic is **restraint before release** — keep verses sparse (`intimate, piano, restrained`) so
the `huge congregational choir` chorus and the `repeated declaration bridge` land like an actual room full
of people. If it stays too small → add `bigger drums, soaring strings, key change final chorus`. If it loses
the intimacy → add `sparse verse, solo piano, breathy vocal`. Want it more anthemic/cinematic → add `epic
strings, cinematic swell, stadium reverb`.

## Variations
- **Female-lead / duet:** swap `passionate lead vocal` → `passionate female lead vocal`, or `worship-leader-and-congregation call and response`.
- **Bigger and more cinematic:** add `orchestral strings, cinematic build, huge stadium reverb`.
- **Acoustic/unplugged (worship-night intimate):** drop the drums, add `acoustic guitar, solo piano, intimate congregation` — no electric build.
- **Companion set:** pairs with the laid-back groove version in `still-cove-forrest-frank-worship.md` — same place, two very different worship lanes (chill groove vs. soaring anthem).
- **Title/hook swaps:** "Be Still," "Still My Soul," "Quiet Before You," "The Water's Calm."
