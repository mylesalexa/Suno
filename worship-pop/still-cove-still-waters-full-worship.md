# Still Cove (Still Waters)

*Christian worship anthem — modern arena-worship (Hillsong-lane) energy confirmed as the right style. Soaring,
congregational, and this time built with **full biblical backing**, mapped the same way as the Truth Has a
Name album: a recommended topic, the reasoning behind it, and every supporting verse named and placed.*

---

## 1. Recommended Christian Topic

**Resting in God's presence, pictured in the still water of the family cove — "He leads me beside still
waters... He restores my soul" (Psalm 23) fused with "Be still, and know that I am God" (Psalm 46:10).**

It fuses the cove's own name into two of Scripture's most direct "stillness" passages: Psalm 23 gives the
*image* (still water, a restored soul, a Shepherd who leads), Psalm 46:10 gives the *declaration* (be still,
know He is God). "Still Cove" isn't just a place name in this song — it's the theology.

## 2. Why This Topic Works

- **It's the most direct scripture match possible.** The cove is *literally* named "Still" — Psalm 23:2's
  "still waters" isn't a stretch, it's the same word. That's a gift most worship songwriters have to invent;
  here it's already sitting in the location's name.
- **It's personal first, universal second.** It's grounded in one real family's real place, but "a still
  place where you finally exhale" is something every listener has (or longs for) — so it sings as *their*
  song too, not just a family's private memory.
- **It's rest, not hype.** Where a lot of modern worship reaches for adrenaline, this one reaches for
  Sabbath — stillness as the posture of trust, not a lack of energy. That's a needed, honest note.
- **It still builds.** Rest doesn't mean small. The song still earns a huge, congregational, key-lifted
  finish — the stillness is the foundation the declaration gets *built* on, not a substitute for it.

## 3. Bible Verses Supporting the Theme

1. **Psalm 23:1–3** — "The Lord is my shepherd... He leads me beside still waters. He restores my soul."
   *(the title image — Verse 1 and the chorus's "still waters" line.)*
2. **Psalm 46:10** — "Be still, and know that I am God." *(the chorus's central declaration.)*
3. **Psalm 65:8** — "You call forth songs of joy where morning dawns and evening fades." *(Verse 1, the
   golden sunset over the water.)*
4. **Psalm 8:3–4** — "When I consider Your heavens, the work of Your fingers... what is man that You are
   mindful of him?" *(Verse 1, the quiet wonder under the sky.)*
5. **Psalm 133:1** — "How good and pleasant it is when God's people live together in unity." *(Verse 2, the
   full dock, always room for one more.)*
6. **Habakkuk 2:20** — "The Lord is in His holy temple; let all the earth be silent before Him." *(the
   bridge's opening call to stillness.)*
7. **Matthew 11:28–29** — "Come to me, all who are weary... and I will give you rest... you will find rest
   for your souls." *(the bridge's "still my soul" — rest as something Christ gives, not self-generated.)*
8. **1 Thessalonians 5:18** — "Give thanks in all circumstances; for this is God's will for you in Christ
   Jesus." *(the whole song's posture — every detail of the day becomes thanks.)*

## 4. Song Title

**"Still Cove (Still Waters)"**
Alt titles: *"Still Waters," "Be Still, Still Cove," "Restore My Soul," "Beside Still Water."*

## 5. Emotional Arc

| Section | Feeling | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Verse 1** | Quiet, restful, awed | Arrive at the cove, the world goes quiet, creation itself testifies |
| **Pre-Chorus** | Surrender, anticipation | No need to say a word — He already knows |
| **Chorus** | Declaration, worship | "Be still and know You're God" — the still water becomes praise |
| **Verse 2** | Warm, grateful | The full dock, the neighbors, a gift nobody earned |
| **Pre-Chorus** | Surrender, anticipation | Repeats, building |
| **Bridge** | Reverent hush → mounting declaration | Silence before God builds into an unrestrained, repeated declaration |
| **Final Chorus** | Communal, soaring | Whole room singing full voice, key lifts |
| **Outro** | Settled, at peace | Quiet fade — "still my soul" |

## 6. Suno-Ready Style Prompt

> Modern arena worship soft-rock, 74 BPM, key of A, soaring electric guitar swells, string pads, driving toms, passionate lead vocal, huge congregational choir, anthemic build, repeated declaration bridge, restful and soaring

**Tempo & vocal feel:** ~**74 BPM** (a building ballad-to-anthem, not a dance groove or EDM drop), key of
**A** (lift for the final chorus). Sparse, restrained verses — piano and voice — swelling into a full-band,
congregational chorus. Lead vocal: warm, intimate on the verses, soaring and full by the bridge; huge
choir/congregation joins in by the final passes of the bridge.
