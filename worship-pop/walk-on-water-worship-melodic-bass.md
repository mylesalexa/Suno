*A worship banger in the **melodic-bass / melodic-dubstep** lane (Seven Lions × Tritonal): a cinematic,

## The style (Seven Lions × Tritonal — researched)
- **Melodic bass / melodic dubstep:** blends **trance melody + dubstep heaviness + cinematic atmosphere**
  (Seven Lions is the pioneer of the lane; Tritonal's brighter festival-progressive sits right beside it).
- **The build:** ethereal, reverb-soaked pads, soft synths, emotional chords — spacious enough for sung-rap verses.
- **The drop:** **soaring supersaw leads** meet a **detuned growl bass** + deep sub — massive, emotional, festival-huge.
- **~140 BPM half-time:** verses breathe at a hip-hop feel; the drop crushes. Cinematic, mystical, epic.

---

## Creative Direction (topic · why it works · scripture · arc)
**Topic:** stepping out in faith when everything says stay in the boat — eyes on Jesus, not the storm.
**Why it works:** the melodic-dubstep **storm-to-breakthrough** arc *is* the story — the ethereal, anxious
breakdown is the wind and the waves; the drop is the moment you step out and don't sink. Fear → faith,
scored by a supersaw sunrise. Testimony, not sermon.

**Bible verses (where each lands):**
- **Matthew 14:22–33** — Peter walks on water, starts to sink, cries out, Jesus catches him ("why did you
  doubt?"). → the whole song (**V1, V2, hook**).
- **Isaiah 43:2** — "when you pass through the waters, I will be with you." → the **bridge**.
- **Psalm 23:4** — "though I walk through the valley... I will fear no evil, for You are with me." → **bridge**.
- **2 Timothy 1:7** — "not a spirit of fear, but of power, love and a sound mind." → **bridge**.
- **1 John 4:18** — "perfect love casts out fear." → **Verse 2**.

**Emotional arc:** *the storm → He calls "come" → I step out → I sink, He catches me → fearless, I walk.*

## ⭐ Best Suno prompt
```
Clean worship melodic bass hip-hop, 140 BPM half-time, A minor, ethereal breakdown, melodic sung-rap verses, massive melodic dubstep drop, supersaw leads, detuned growl bass, sub, big sung hook
```
**Tip:** the Seven Lions magic is the **contrast** — keep `cinematic ethereal breakdown` (the storm) against
the `massive melodic dubstep drop, detuned growl bass` (the payoff). `140 BPM half-time` keeps the sung-rap
verses tuneful, not busy. Keep it **melodic**, not brostep — if the drop gets too harsh add `melodic, pretty
supersaw lead`. Want more trance (Tritonal) → add `soaring trance lead, euphoric`; more heavy → add `heavier growl, dubstep`.

- **Heavier melodic dubstep:** add `heavier growl bass, harder drop, dubstep wobble`.
