# Wide Open *(clean worship hip-hop banger — narrow road, open invitation)*

*A clean **worship hip-hop banger** in the Golden Moments / clean-hip-hop lane — melodic sung-rap verses,
808 bounce, gospel-soul chords, big anthemic sung hook and congregation vocals. The message: **the world is
turning away, but I'm turning toward You** — the road is narrow, but the door is **wide open**. Loving and
welcoming, never preachy or condemning: come as you are, all are welcome, the invitation stays open. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief / direction:** more hip-hop than "Every Good Gift" (like the Golden Moments/Faloma lane). Theme:
> the world turns away, I turn toward God; the narrow road — but framed as **loving invitation, not
> condemnation.** All are welcome, the door is open. Rooted in scripture. Publish-ready. In `worship-pop/`.

---

## Creative Direction (topic · why it works · scripture · arc)
**Topic:** choosing to turn toward God while the culture turns away — and preaching it as an *open door*, not
a closed one. **Why it works:** the narrow road usually sounds exclusive; here it's reframed as the *most
welcoming* thing — "the road is narrow but the door's wide open, come as you are." A hip-hop banger carrying
grace, not guilt.

**Bible verses (where each lands):**
- **Romans 12:2** — "Do not conform to the pattern of this world, but be transformed." → **Verse 1** (the turning).
- **Joshua 24:15** — "As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord." → **Verse 1**.
- **Matthew 7:13–14** — the narrow gate/road (accessible to all, a choice not a condemnation). → **hook**.
- **Matthew 11:28** — "Come to me, all who are weary and burdened." → **Verse 2** ("come weary, come heavy").
- **Revelation 22:17 / Isaiah 55:1** — "Let the one who is thirsty come... take the water of life freely." → **hook / Verse 2**.
- **John 6:37** — "whoever comes to me I will never cast out." → **Verse 2** ("no soul too far gone").
- **Revelation 3:20** — "I stand at the door and knock." → **Verse 2**.
- **Luke 15** — the prodigal; the Father runs to meet him. → **bridge**.

**Emotional arc:** *the world's noise → I turn → the door's open → come home (all are welcome).*

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** clean **worship hip-hop** — 808 bounce, warm gospel-soul chords, claps; **melodic sung-rap
verses** into a huge anthemic sung hook with a soaring synth lift and **congregation/choir gang vocals**.
A banger with grace — hands-up, soulful, radio-and-youth-service polished. Clean, never condemning.

- **Tempo:** ~**104 BPM** — head-nod hip-hop bounce with a big lift on the hook (the Golden Moments pocket).
- **Key:** **A minor** (soulful, with a bright euphoric lift on the chorus).
- **Vocal:** confident **male** — melodic sung-rap on verses (tuneful, lots of pitch movement), big and
  anthemic on the hook, stacked **congregation/choir** vocals. Light auto-tune fine. Clean. (Female / duet noted.)

**Tip:** `melodic sung-rap verses` + `huge anthemic sung hook` keeps it tuneful and worshipful (not a hard
trap record); `gospel-soul chords` + `congregation gang vocals` give it church weight. Keep the message
grace-forward — the hook does the inviting. Want more CHH bounce → add `hard 808, trap-soul`; more anthem →
add `choir, key change final chorus`; more EDM (like Every Good Gift) → add `festival synth drop, sidechain`.

- **More EDM (pairs with Every Good Gift):** add `festival synth drop, four-on-the-floor lift`.
- **Fuller gospel:** add `hammond organ, live drums, full gospel choir` for a Sunday-morning build.
- **Softer/intimate open:** start V1 `(sparse, just 808 and organ)`, bring the choir in at chorus 1.
