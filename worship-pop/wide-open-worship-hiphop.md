# Wide Open *(worship hip-hop × EDM × punk rock — Tritonal festival banger)*

*The fusion rebuild: **hip-hop meets EDM meets punk rock** — melodic sung-rap verses over distorted
power-chord guitars and punchy punk drums, exploding into a **Tritonal-style festival supersaw drop**, with
a super-catchy **gang-shout "wide open!" hook**. Gospel vibe removed (no organ, no choir). Message intact:
**the world is turning away, but I'm turning toward You** — narrow road, but the door's wide open. Loving,
welcoming, never condemning. Clean, euphoric, singable. Cloudlight Proxy.*

> **Brief / the change:** kill the gospel vibe, go **more EDM (Tritonal)**, keep it a **catchy, singable**
> banger, and fuse **hip-hop EDM with punk rock**. Same open-invitation message and scripture, new sound:
> punk guitars + festival supersaw drops + rap-sung verses + shout-along hook.

---

## Creative Direction (topic · why it works · scripture · arc)
**Topic:** turning toward God while the culture turns away — preached as an *open door*, not a closed one.
**Why it works now:** the punk-rock defiance ("I'm turning around") + the euphoric EDM drop + a chant hook =
a rebellious, hands-up anthem that still *invites* instead of condemns. Grace with distortion and a drop.

**Bible verses (where each lands):**
- **Romans 12:2** — "Do not conform to the pattern of this world." → **Verse 1** (the turning).
- **Joshua 24:15** — "As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord." → **Verse 1**.
- **Matthew 7:13–14** — the narrow gate/road (accessible to all — a choice, not a condemnation). → **hook**.
- **Matthew 11:28** — "Come to me, all who are weary and burdened." → **Verse 2**.
- **Revelation 22:17 / Isaiah 55:1** — "let the one who is thirsty come... water of life freely." → **hook / Verse 2**.
- **John 6:37** — "whoever comes to me I will never cast out." → **Verse 2**.
- **Revelation 3:20** — "I stand at the door and knock." → **Verse 2**.
- **Luke 15** — the prodigal; the Father runs to meet him. → **bridge**.

**Emotional arc:** *the world's noise → I turn (defiant) → the drop, door wide open → come home.*

## Style / Tempo / Vocal
**Genre:** clean **worship hip-hop × EDM × punk rock** — distorted power-chord guitars + punchy punk drums
under **melodic sung-rap verses**, exploding into a **festival supersaw drop** (sidechain pump, deep 808);
huge **gang-shout hook**, call-and-response chants. Rebellious, euphoric, hands-up. No gospel/organ/choir.

- **Tempo:** ~**150 BPM half-time** — punk drive in the verses, full festival future-bass on the drop.
- **Key:** **A minor** (emotional, euphoric lift on the drop).
- **Vocal:** passionate **male** — melodic sung-rap on verses (tuneful, lots of pitch movement), big
  **gang-shout** on the hook, punk-anthem call-and-response. Light auto-tune fine. Clean. (Female/duet noted.)

`gritty guitars, raw`; not enough drop → add `bigger supersaw drop, wobble sub`; more punk → push `double-time punk drums`.
